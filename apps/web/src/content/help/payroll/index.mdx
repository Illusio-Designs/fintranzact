Payroll keeps your employees' records, marks their attendance and leave, works out each month's salary and posts it to your books. It is a **paid add-on**: it is not part of your plan, and organisations in the Full Access Trial can try it with up to **10 employees**.

**The Payroll add-on is not on sale yet.** Until it is released, the Payroll page shows a notice that explains this and has no buy button. Organisations that already have the add-on (for example from the trial) use everything described here.

## What Payroll does

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Employees** | Keep each person's details, PAN, Aadhaar, UAN, ESIC number, bank account and exit. See [Employees](/help/payroll/employees) |
| **Salary structures** | Set up salary components and templates, and give each employee an annual CTC that becomes monthly amounts. See [Salary structures](/help/payroll/salary-structures) |
| **Attendance** | Mark each day, keep the holiday calendar, weekly offs and shifts. See [Attendance and leave](/help/payroll/attendance-and-leave) |
| **Leave** | Leave types (CL, SL, EL, LOP), balances, applications and encashment. See [Attendance and leave](/help/payroll/attendance-and-leave) |
| **Payroll runs** | Each month: lock attendance, calculate, review, approve, post to the books and pay. See [Running payroll](/help/payroll/running-payroll) |
| **Payslips** | A PDF for every employee once the run is approved, by download or email. See [Payslips and the bank file](/help/payroll/payslips) |
| **Statutory settings** | Which schemes you are registered for, and every rate, limit, slab and due date for each financial year. See [Statutory settings](/help/payroll/statutory-settings) |
| **Statutory dues** | What you owe PF, ESI, professional tax, labour welfare fund and TDS, and recording what you paid with the challan details. See [Statutory files and registers](/help/payroll/statutory-files-and-registers) |
| **Filings and registers** | The PF ECR file, the ESIC file, professional tax sheets, Form 24Q data, a Form 16 working copy and the wages, attendance, leave, bonus and gratuity registers. See [Statutory files and registers](/help/payroll/statutory-files-and-registers) |
| **Bonus** | A yearly bonus run from approved payroll, with the ceilings and percentage you set. See [Bonus](/help/payroll/bonus) |
| **Gratuity** | What gratuity would be payable today, and an optional provision. See [Gratuity](/help/payroll/gratuity) |
| **Loans and advances** | Issue a loan or advance, recover it through payroll, part-pay, foreclose, skip. See [Loans and advances](/help/payroll/loans-and-advances) |
| **Full and final** | Settle an employee who left: leave encashment, gratuity, bonus due, recoveries and loan balances, and relieving letters. See [Full and final settlement](/help/payroll/full-and-final), [Relieving letters](/help/payroll/relieving-letters) and [Labour-law registers](/help/payroll/labour-law-registers) |
| **Employee app** | Invite employees to their own login to check in with a selfie, see attendance and payslips, see their own loans and advances (read only), apply for leave and download Form 16. See [Employee access, check-ins and device import](/help/payroll/employee-access-and-check-in) and [Using the employee app](/help/payroll/employee-guide) |

## Who can use it

- **Owners and admins** can do everything, including approving a run and seeing full PAN, Aadhaar and bank numbers.
- **Accountants** (bookkeeping) can prepare payroll: add employees, mark attendance, calculate, post and mark paid. They cannot approve a run, delete a template or see full identity numbers (they see them partly hidden).
- **Every other role** (sellers, seller managers, and the read-only and filing accountant roles) cannot see salary data at all.

## Statutory payroll

If your business is registered, payroll calculates **provident fund, ESI, professional tax, labour welfare fund and income-tax TDS** in each run from rates you keep per financial year, books them to their own payable accounts and gives you the files for your CA or the portals. Nothing is switched on until you register the business in [Statutory settings](/help/payroll/statutory-settings): a business without PF, ESI or TDS runs payroll exactly as before. See [PF and ESI](/help/payroll/pf-and-esi), [Professional tax and LWF](/help/payroll/professional-tax-and-lwf) and [TDS on salary](/help/payroll/tds-on-salary).

**Fintranzact files nothing and pays nothing** with EPFO, ESIC, the income-tax portal or a state. Bonus, gratuity, loans and advances, full and final settlement and relieving letters are year-end and exit work (described below): every figure in them is yours and your CA's to confirm.

The Labour Codes and all the rates in payroll change; **have your CA confirm** the salary structure, the overtime rate and every statutory figure before you rely on them.
