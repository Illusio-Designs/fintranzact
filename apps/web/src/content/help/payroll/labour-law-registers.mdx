Open **Payroll → Filings and registers**, **Registers**. Alongside the wages, attendance, leave, bonus and gratuity registers, four more are made from data you already keep:

| Register | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Register of employees (employment)** | Every employee: father or spouse, gender, date of birth, designation, department, type, joining date, status, last working day and reason for leaving |
| **Register of deductions, fines and advances** | For the financial year: deductions from wages other than PF, ESI, professional tax, labour welfare fund and TDS, plus loans and advances given and the amounts recovered |
| **Register of overtime** | Overtime hours and pay for each employee and month of approved payroll |
| **Register of full and final settlements** | Each settlement: amounts due, recoveries, net payable, status and the date paid |

They download as CSV files by default. Choose **Download as: PDF (landscape, for printing)** above the buttons to get any register, these four and the wages, attendance, leave, bonus and gratuity registers, as a PDF instead. The financial year is the one chosen above the buttons (the employment register has no year).

The PDF is a landscape A4 table with your business name, the register and its period and the column headings on every page, page numbers, and the label **Working copy for CA / legal review. Formats vary by state.** A long list simply runs on to more pages. A register with too many columns for one page (a wages register with many allowances, or the attendance register) is printed in groups of columns, each group repeating the employee code and name, so no figure is squeezed or cut. A very long text value is shortened with ... in the PDF; the CSV always has everything in full.

**These are working copies.** The registers a state or an Act (Shops and Establishments, Contract Labour and others) asks for have different formats and fields, and Fintranzact does not claim any of these is the statutory form or that using them makes you compliant. Give them to your CA or lawyer to arrange in the form your state needs.

The registers are built from your approved payroll, so a month without an approved run has no rows.
