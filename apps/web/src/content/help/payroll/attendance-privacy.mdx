When an employee checks in or out with the app, the following is collected.

| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| The time (the server's clock) | Attendance | The employee, and payroll and HR |
| A selfie, when the business asks for one | To show who checked in | Only owners, admins and HR, to review a check-in |
| One location reading, when the business uses a location rule | To compare with the allowed workplaces | Only owners, admins and HR |
| A device id the app makes up | To spot repeated use of one phone | Payroll and HR |

## What is not done

- **No tracking.** The location is read once per check-in or check-out, only while the app is open. There is no background or live tracking.
- **Not shared with the AI assistant.** Selfies, locations and check-in records are never given to the AI assistant or to any AI tool, and are never written to logs.
- **Not shown to other employees,** and not part of payslips or reports.
- **No public links.** A selfie is shown only inside a signed-in review screen.

## How long selfies are kept

Selfies are deleted automatically after the retention period the business sets, **90 days by default** (between 7 and 365). Shortening the period deletes older selfies straight away. The times and the attendance they produced are kept as attendance records. A rejected or approved check-in keeps its flags but not its photo once the period has passed.

## Consent

Before the first check-in the employee sees this list and must agree. The agreement is saved with a version number and date, and is asked again if the wording changes. A business may wish to have its own attendance policy and consent wording reviewed by its CA or legal adviser.

## Location limits

A phone can report a false location, and the app cannot always tell. Location results are supporting evidence for HR to review, not proof.
