A **stock transfer** moves goods from one of your [warehouses](/help/inventory/warehouses) to another, for example from the godown to the shop. Stock leaves the source warehouse and arrives in the destination at the same moment. Your total stock doesn't change, so a transfer has no effect on your accounts.

Open it from the sidebar: **Inventory → Stock Transfers**.

## Making a transfer

1. Click **+ New transfer**.
2. Choose the **From** warehouse and the **To** warehouse. Only active warehouses are listed, and each list hides the warehouse picked in the other. Your sales default warehouse is marked **(default)**.
3. Check the **Date**. It defaults to today and can be changed.
4. Add the items to move:
   - Search for an item. Once **From** is chosen, each result shows how much is available there ("12 pcs here").
   - Enter the **Quantity** to move. The line shows how much is "in this warehouse".
   - Click **Add item** for more lines, or the delete icon to remove one.
5. Click **Transfer N items**.

You see "Stock transferred" when it's done. The button stays disabled until both warehouses and at least one item with a quantity are filled in. Lines left without an item or with a zero quantity are ignored.

Items with variants appear once per variant (for example "T-shirt — Red / M"), so you transfer each variant separately. Service items can't be transferred, since they don't carry stock.

For an item that [tracks batches](/help/inventory/batches-and-expiry), pick the batch to move under the line, or leave it on **Earliest expiry first**. The batch keeps its number and expiry in the destination, and the journal shows it next to the item. A batch can't be transferred beyond what it holds at the source.

## Rules

| Rule | What you see if it's broken |
|---|---|
| From and To must be different | "Pick two different warehouses" |
| Both warehouses must be active | "That warehouse is inactive" |
| Quantities must be more than zero, with up to 3 decimals | "Transfer quantities must be more than zero" |
| 1 to 100 lines per transfer | — |
| The source must have enough stock | "Not enough stock to transfer: N available" |

The stock check always applies, whatever your **Selling more than you have** setting is. You can't transfer stock you don't have. If the same item is on two lines, both lines are added together for the check.

A transfer is all-or-nothing: if any line fails, nothing moves.

## Who can transfer

- **Owners and admins** can transfer between any warehouses.
- **Seller managers** can transfer when they have the **Transfer** permission on *both* warehouses. Set this on each warehouse's **Access** tab (see [Warehouses](/help/inventory/warehouses)).
- **Sellers and accountants** can't make transfers.

## The transfer journal

The Stock Transfers page lists every transfer, newest first, 25 per page:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Transfer date |
| **From → To** | The two warehouses |
| **Items** | Each item and quantity moved, with a count when there are several |
| **Quantity** | Total quantity moved |

Transfers also appear in the [Stock Ledger](/help/inventory/reports) as "Transfer in" and "Transfer out".

:::caution[Transfers can't be edited or deleted]
Once saved, a transfer is permanent. There's no edit, cancel or delete. If you moved the wrong item or quantity, make a second transfer in the opposite direction.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there an "in transit" stage, where goods have left one godown but not reached the other?**
No. A transfer is instant: the stock is out of the source and in the destination as soon as you save. If you want to see goods on the road, create a warehouse with the type **In transit** and make two transfers (godown → in transit when the truck leaves, in transit → shop when it arrives).

**Do transfers have a number, like TR-001?**
No. Transfers are identified by their date, warehouses and items in the journal.

**Can I transfer to a specific rack or bin?**
No. Transfers work at warehouse level. [Locations](/help/inventory/warehouses) are labels only for now.

**Does a transfer affect my P&L or GST?**
No. It only changes where your stock is. Your total quantity and stock value stay the same.
