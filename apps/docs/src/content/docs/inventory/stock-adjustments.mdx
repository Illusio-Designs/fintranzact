A **stock adjustment** changes your stock without a sale or purchase: goods that got damaged, expired, went missing, turned up during a count, or were given away as samples. Every adjustment records the warehouse, the reason, the date and who made it.

Open it from the sidebar: **Inventory → Stock Adjustments**.

## Making an adjustment

1. Click **+ New adjustment**.
2. Choose the **Warehouse**. Only active warehouses are listed. Your default warehouse is marked **(default)**.
3. Choose **Remove stock** (the default) or **Add stock**. This applies to every line in the adjustment.
4. Choose a **Reason**:
   - Damaged goods
   - Expired
   - Lost or stolen
   - Found extra stock
   - Opening stock
   - Sample or free issue
   - Other: you must then fill in **Describe the reason** (for example "Used for repairs").
5. Check the **Date**. It defaults to today. You can pick an earlier date to record something that happened in the past.
6. Add the items:
   - Search for an item. Each result shows how much is at the chosen warehouse. Items with variants are listed once per variant.
   - Enter the quantity to add or remove, always as a positive number.
   - Click **+ Add item** for more lines.
7. Click **Remove stock** or **Add stock** to save.

You see "Stock adjusted" when it's saved. Lines with no item or a zero quantity are ignored. An adjustment can have up to 100 lines.

:::caution[You can't remove more than the warehouse holds]
When removing stock, Fintranzact checks each item at the chosen warehouse. If you try to remove more than is there, you see "Only N in stock at this warehouse" and nothing is saved. This applies whatever your **Selling more than you have** setting is.
:::

## The adjustments list

The page lists adjustments newest first, 25 per page. Two tabs:

- **All adjustments**: everything, including adjustments made from an item's **Adjust Stock** button.
- **From physical counts**: only adjustments posted from a [physical stock count](/inventory/physical-stock).

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Adjustment date |
| **Item** | Item (and variant) |
| **Warehouse** | Where the stock was added or removed |
| **Change** | Quantity added (green, +) or removed (red, −) |
| **Stock after** | The item's **total** stock across all warehouses right after the adjustment was saved |
| **Reason** | The reason you chose or typed |
| **By** | The team member who made it |

:::note[Correcting a mistake]
Adjustments can't be edited or deleted. To undo one, make a new adjustment in the opposite direction with a reason such as "Other: reversing wrong entry of 12 March".
:::

## Adjusting from the item itself

Each product also has an **Adjust Stock** button in its detail panel (**Inventory → Stock Items** → click the item). This is a quick way to fix one item:

- Choose **Add Stock** or **Remove Stock**, enter a **Quantity** and an **Adjustment Date**, and optionally a **Reason**.
- For items with variants, pick the **Variant** first.
- The panel shows the current stock, a "New stock will be…" preview, and the last 10 adjustments.

The quick panel has no warehouse picker. It always adjusts the **Opening stock & adjustments** default warehouse (see [Default warehouses](/inventory/warehouses#default-warehouses)). It also doesn't stop you from going below zero. For anything involving a specific godown, use the Stock Adjustments page instead.

In the web app, an item's **Current Stock** can't be typed over once the item exists. If a stock number is edited directly (for example through the API), the difference is recorded as an adjustment with the reason "Stock edited on item".

## Who can adjust stock

- **Owners and admins** can adjust stock in any warehouse.
- **Seller managers** need the **Adjust & count** permission on the warehouse's **Access** tab (see [Warehouses](/inventory/warehouses#access)). Without it they see "You don't have adjustment permission for this warehouse".
- **Sellers and accountants** can't adjust stock.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I record the value of damaged goods?**
Adjustments record quantities only. The value of your remaining stock is worked out by the [stock valuation](/inventory/stock-valuation) method, so removing damaged goods automatically lowers your closing stock value.

**Should I use "Opening stock" as the reason when migrating?**
You can, but the simplest way to enter opening stock is the stock quantity when creating each item, or an item import. That stock is valued at the item's purchase price. Stock added through an adjustment is valued at your purchase-bill average instead, falling back to the purchase price only when there are no bills.

**Why does "Stock after" not match the warehouse's stock?**
"Stock after" is the item's total across all warehouses, not just the one on that row. Use the [Stock Ledger](/inventory/reports#stock-ledger) filtered to a warehouse to see that warehouse's running balance.

**Can I adjust stock into a rack or bin?**
No. Adjustments work at warehouse level.
