Payroll keeps your employees' records, marks their attendance and leave, works out each month's salary and posts it to your books. It is a **paid add-on**: it is not part of your plan, and organisations in the Full Access Trial can try it with up to **10 employees**.

**The Payroll add-on is not on sale yet.** Until it is released, the Payroll page shows a notice that explains this and has no buy button. Organisations that already have the add-on (for example from the trial) use everything described here.

## What Payroll does

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Employees** | Keep each person's details, PAN, Aadhaar, UAN, ESIC number, bank account and exit. See [Employees](/help/payroll/employees) |
| **Salary structures** | Set up salary components and templates, and give each employee an annual CTC that becomes monthly amounts. See [Salary structures](/help/payroll/salary-structures) |
| **Attendance** | Mark each day, keep the holiday calendar, weekly offs and shifts. See [Attendance and leave](/help/payroll/attendance-and-leave) |
| **Leave** | Leave types (CL, SL, EL, LOP), balances, applications and encashment. See [Attendance and leave](/help/payroll/attendance-and-leave) |
| **Payroll runs** | Each month: lock attendance, calculate, review, approve, post to the books and pay. See [Running payroll](/help/payroll/running-payroll) |
| **Payslips** | A PDF for every employee once the run is approved, by download or email. See [Payslips and the bank file](/help/payroll/payslips) |

## Who can use it

- **Owners and admins** can do everything, including approving a run and seeing full PAN, Aadhaar and bank numbers.
- **Accountants** (bookkeeping) can prepare payroll: add employees, mark attendance, calculate, post and mark paid. They cannot approve a run, delete a template or see full identity numbers (they see them partly hidden).
- **Every other role** (sellers, seller managers, and the read-only and filing accountant roles) cannot see salary data at all.

## What Payroll does not do yet

Provident fund (PF), ESI, professional tax and income-tax TDS are **not calculated** in this first release, and there are no PF, ESI or TDS filings. If you deduct them, enter them as manual deductions on the run (see [Running payroll](/help/payroll/running-payroll)). The statutory modules are planned as the next release.

The Labour Codes and all the rates in payroll change; **have your CA confirm** the salary structure, the overtime rate and the figures before you rely on them.
