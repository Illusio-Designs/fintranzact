The **Input Tax Credit** page keeps a ledger of the GST you paid on purchases. Each entry has a status (available, blocked, reversed and so on). The page also warns you about unpaid bills that are close to the 180-day limit, lets you record how the credit was set off against output tax, and lays the figures out in the GSTR-3B Table 4 format.

:::note[Web and desktop only]
The ITC page is available in the web app and the desktop app. The mobile app doesn't have it.
:::

## Where to find it

In the sidebar, under **Reports**, click **Input Tax Credit**. The menu item appears only for GST-registered businesses. Superadmins, Admins and Accountants can view the page and act on it. Sellers can't see it.

The page has five tabs: **Dashboard**, **Ledger**, **Aging Alerts**, **Utilization** and **GSTR-3B Table 4**. The month and year selectors under the tabs choose the **return period** (shown as *"Return period: April 2026"*). Every tab except **Aging Alerts** uses it.

## How ITC entries are created

You never add ITC entries by hand. One entry is created automatically when you save a **purchase invoice** that has GST on it:

- **Return period**: the month of the purchase invoice date.
- **Tax split**: if your business state code equals the supplier's state code, the tax is split equally into CGST and SGST. Otherwise it's all IGST. Cess is recorded as 0.
- **Reverse charge**: the entry is marked **RCM** if the purchase invoice is marked reverse charge.
- **Status**: **Available**.

No entry is created when:

- the purchase invoice has no tax, or
- the business's GST registration type is **Composition**, because composition dealers can't claim ITC.

When a purchase invoice is **cancelled** or **deleted**, its entry changes to **Reversed**, with the reason *invoice cancelled*.

:::caution
The ITC entry is created once, when the purchase invoice is first saved. If you later edit the invoice's amounts, date or supplier, the ITC entry isn't updated. To correct an entry, cancel the purchase invoice and create a new one.
:::

## ITC statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Credit you can claim. Every entry starts here. |
| **Blocked** | You marked it ineligible under Section 17(5) |
| **Reversed** | The purchase invoice was cancelled or deleted |
| **Utilized** | Listed as a filter and summary card. Nothing in the app sets this status at present. |
| **Reclaimed** | Listed as a filter. Nothing in the app sets this status at present. |

## Dashboard

The Dashboard has four cards for the selected period, each with its CGST, SGST and IGST breakdown:

- **Available ITC**
- **Utilized**
- **Reversed**
- **Blocked (Sec 17(5))**

Below the cards:

- **Critical Aging Alerts** shows up to three invoices older than 180 days, with a **View All** link to the Aging Alerts tab.
- **Utilization Summary** appears once you've recorded utilization for the period.

## Ledger

The Ledger lists the ITC entries for the selected period, newest first, 25 per page. Use the status dropdown (**All Statuses**, **Available**, **Utilized**, **Reversed**, **Blocked**, **Reclaimed**) to filter it.

The columns are **Invoice #**, **Party**, **Invoice Date**, **Period**, **Status**, **CGST**, **SGST**, **IGST**, **Total** and **RCM**. An **RCM** badge marks reverse-charge entries.

### Blocking credit (Section 17(5))

1. Hover over an **Available** entry and click **Block**.
2. In **Block ITC — Section 17(5)**, choose a **Block Reason**:

| Reason |
|---|
| Motor Vehicle |
| Food & Beverage |
| Personal Use |
| Club Membership |
| Travel Benefits |
| Works Contract |
| Construction |
| Telecom |
| Other |

3. Optionally add **Notes (optional)**.
4. Click **Block**.

The entry moves to **Blocked** and no longer counts as available.

### Unblocking

Hover over a **Blocked** entry, click **Unblock** and confirm. The entry returns to **Available**, and its block reason is cleared.

## Aging Alerts

Under Section 16(2), you lose the credit on a bill you haven't paid within 180 days of the invoice date. This tab lists every **Available** ITC entry whose purchase invoice is **more than 150 days old** and still has an **unpaid balance**. It covers all periods, not just the selected month.

| Urgency | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Warning** | 150 to 180 days since the invoice date |
| **Critical** | More than 180 days |

Summary boxes show **Total Alerts**, **Critical (>180 days)** and **Warning (150–180 days)**. When there are critical alerts, a red banner shows the total ITC at risk. The table lists **Invoice #**, **Party**, **Invoice Date**, **Days Outstanding**, **ITC at Risk** and **Urgency**, with critical rows first.

To clear an alert, record the payment against the purchase invoice (see [Payments](/payments)). Once the invoice is fully paid it drops off the list.

## Utilization

The Utilization tab records how the period's credit was set off against output tax. This is normally the set-off you make on the GST portal when filing GSTR-3B.

1. Check **Available ITC Balance — *month year***. It shows the available CGST, SGST and IGST and the **Total Available**.
2. Under **Record Utilization**, enter the amounts:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CGST Utilized** | CGST credit used against CGST liability |
| **SGST Utilized** | SGST credit used against SGST liability |
| **IGST → IGST** | IGST credit used against IGST liability |
| **IGST → CGST** | IGST credit used against CGST liability |
| **IGST → SGST** | IGST credit used against SGST liability |
| **Notes (optional)** | Free text |

3. Click **Save Utilization**.

The form reminds you of the prescribed order: *"IGST credit must be used first against IGST liability, then CGST, then SGST."* Fintranzact doesn't enforce that order. It only checks that:

- **CGST Utilized** doesn't exceed available CGST,
- **SGST Utilized** doesn't exceed available SGST, and
- the three IGST amounts together don't exceed available IGST.

### The journal entry

Saving also posts a **system journal entry**, dated today, with the narration *"ITC utilization for return period YYYY-MM"*:

| Utilization | Debit (output tax) | Credit (input tax) |
|---|---|---|
| CGST | Output CGST (2100) | Input CGST (1510) |
| SGST | Output SGST (2101) | Input SGST (1511) |
| IGST → IGST | Output IGST (2102) | Input IGST (1512) |
| IGST → CGST | Output CGST (2100) | Input IGST (1512) |
| IGST → SGST | Output SGST (2101) | Input IGST (1512) |

Those six accounts must exist in your chart of accounts. If one is missing, the save fails with *"Chart of accounts entry with code … not found."*

:::caution
Each period has a single utilization record, and saving again overwrites it. **Each save also posts a new journal entry, though**, and the previous one isn't reversed. If you need to correct a period's figures, delete or reverse the earlier journal entry yourself (see [Journal Entries](/accounting/journal-entries)).

Recording utilization doesn't change the status of ledger entries. They stay **Available**, and the **Utilized** card doesn't move.
:::

**Current Period Utilization** at the bottom shows the amounts saved for the period.

## GSTR-3B Table 4

This tab lays out the period's ITC in the same rows as GSTR-3B Table 4, with **Integrated Tax**, **Central Tax**, **State/UT Tax** and **Cess** columns.

| Row | How Fintranzact fills it |
|---|---|
| 4(A)(1) Import of goods | Always ₹0 (imports aren't tracked) |
| 4(A)(2) Import of services | Always ₹0 |
| 4(A)(3) Inward supplies liable to reverse charge | **Available** entries marked RCM |
| 4(A)(5) All other ITC | **Available** entries not marked RCM |
| 4(B)(1) As per Rules 42 & 43 | Entries reversed with reason Rule 42 or 43. The app has no way to set these reasons, so this row stays ₹0. |
| 4(B)(2) Others (Section 17(5)) | **Blocked** entries |
| 4(C) Net ITC Available (A – B) | 4(A) total minus 4(B) total |
| 4(D)(1) As per Section 17(5) | **Blocked** entries |
| 4(D)(2) Others | **Reversed** entries (for example, cancelled purchase invoices) |

:::caution[Check 4(C) before filing]
Blocked entries are already left out of 4(A), which only counts **Available** entries. They are then subtracted again in 4(B)(2). So when the period has blocked credit, **4(C) is lower than the true net ITC by the blocked amount**. Work out net ITC as 4(A) total, or adjust 4(C), before copying the figures to the portal.
:::

### How this differs from the GSTR-3B tab

**GST Returns → GSTR-3B** also shows ITC, in section *"4 — Eligible input tax credit"*. That figure is calculated directly from the month's non-cancelled purchase invoices. It doesn't read the ITC ledger, so it **includes** credit you've blocked here. The two screens can therefore show different amounts. For a return that reflects your block decisions, use this page.

<ForBusiness>
**What matters most for an owner:** the **Aging Alerts** tab. Every bill on it is credit you'll lose, or have already lost, unless you pay the supplier. Clear the **Critical** ones first.
</ForBusiness>

<ForCA>
:::tip[Using the ITC page for a monthly 3B]
1. Reconcile [GSTR-2B](/gst/gstr2b) first, and book any **Not in Books** invoices so that their ITC entries exist.
2. On the **Ledger**, **Block** the Section 17(5) items (motor vehicles, food, personal use and so on).
3. Review **GSTR-3B Table 4**. Remember that 4(A)(1)/(2) are always zero and that 4(C) double-counts blocked credit.
4. After filing, record the portal set-off on **Utilization** once. Saving again posts another journal entry.
5. Check **Aging Alerts** for Section 16(2) reversals. The app doesn't reverse aged credit automatically.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is there no ITC entry for my purchase invoice?**
Entries are created only for purchase invoices with GST, and only if the business isn't registered under Composition. Check the invoice's tax amount and the business's GST registration type.

**I corrected a purchase invoice, but the ITC ledger still shows the old tax. Why?**
The ITC entry is written once, when the invoice is created, and later edits don't update it. Cancel the invoice (its ITC is reversed) and create a corrected one.

**The Utilized card shows ₹0 even after I saved utilization. Is something wrong?**
No. Saving utilization records the set-off and posts the journal entry, but it doesn't change individual ledger entries to Utilized. The saved amounts appear under **Utilization Summary** and **Current Period Utilization**.

**Why does the GSTR-3B tab show more ITC than this page?**
The GSTR-3B tab under GST Returns calculates ITC straight from purchase invoices and ignores blocks. This page uses the ITC ledger, which reflects your blocks and reversals.

**Can I block only part of an invoice's credit?**
No. Blocking applies to the whole ITC entry for that purchase invoice.

**Does an aging alert reverse my ITC automatically?**
No. The alert is a warning only, and the entry stays **Available**. Pay the supplier, or deal with the reversal when you file.
