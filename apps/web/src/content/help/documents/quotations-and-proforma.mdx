A **quotation** tells a customer what you would charge. A **proforma invoice** looks like an invoice and is usually sent to ask for an advance before you deliver. In Fintranzact both work the same way:

- They are always on the **sales** side.
- They do **not** change stock, do **not** add to the customer's ledger, and do **not** appear in GST reports.
- You can **convert** either one into a tax invoice.

| | Quotation | Proforma Invoice |
|---|---|---|
| Default number | `QTN-00001` | `PI-00001` |
| Statuses | Draft, Sent, Cancelled | Draft, Sent, Cancelled |
| Converts into | Invoice | Invoice |

## Creating a quotation or proforma

<ForDesktop>
1. In the sidebar, under **VOUCHERS**, click **Quotations** (or **Proforma Invoices**).
2. Click **+ New Quotation** (or **+ New Proforma**). The **New Quotation** panel slides in.
3. Pick the **Customer**. Type to search, or choose **Create customer** to add a new one on the spot.
4. Set the **Date** and **Due date**. The due date fills in from the customer's credit period (7 days if none is set) until you change it yourself. For a quotation, use it as the "valid until" date.
5. Add **Line Items**: pick a product or type a custom item name, then set quantity, unit, price, tax % and discount %. Each line can also carry its own notes.
6. Optionally add charges (**Shipping** or **+ Add charge**), a **Round Off**, **Notes** and **Terms & conditions**. The terms start from your business's default terms.
7. Click **Create Quotation** (or **Create Proforma Invoice**).

If the customer is on a price level, prices for picked items come from that level. Prices you type yourself are kept.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
1. Tap **More** → **Quotations** (or **Proforma invoices**).
2. Tap the **+** button. The **New Quotation** screen opens.
3. Tap **Select customer...** and pick a customer.
4. Set the **Quotation Date** (or **Proforma Date**) and **Valid Until**.
5. Tap **Add Item** for each line and fill in the item, quantity, price, tax and discount.
6. Optionally add **Notes (optional)**.
7. Tap **Create Quotation** (or **Create Proforma Invoice**).

The mobile form has no charges, round-off or separate terms field. Use the web app if you need them.
</ForMobile>

:::caution
On these documents the **Discount** box at the bottom of the web form (the whole-document ₹ / % discount) is **not saved** — only line-level discount % is kept. Likewise, **Round Off** is only added to the saved total when the document also has at least one charge. Check the total in the detail panel after saving, and use line discounts instead of a document discount.
:::

## Working with the list

<ForDesktop>
The list has status tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Sent** and **Cancelled**, and the page search looks for a party name or number. Columns are **Party**, **Quotation #** (or **Proforma #**), **Date**, **Due Date**, **Status** and **Total**.

Hover over a row to see its actions:

| Action | When it shows | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Mark Sent** | Draft | Changes the status to Sent |
| **Convert to Invoice** | Always | Creates a sale invoice from this document and takes you to **Invoices** |
| **Delete** | Draft | Deletes the document after you confirm (Admin or Owner only) |

Click a row to open the detail panel. It shows the party and phone, status, date, due date, items, totals, notes and terms, and a **Share link** section. Drafts have **Edit** and **Delete** buttons at the bottom.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The screen has status chips **All**, **Draft**, **Sent** and **Cancelled**, and a search bar that matches the number or party. Each card shows the number, party, date, total and status.

| Action | When it shows | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **To Invoice** | Not cancelled | Asks you to confirm, then creates a sale invoice and shows its number |
| **Mark Sent** | Draft | Changes the status to Sent |
| **Delete** | Draft | Deletes after you confirm (Admin or Owner only) |
| **Cancel** | Sent | Cancels the document after you confirm |

Quotations and proformas have no detail screen on mobile, and cannot be edited there.
</ForMobile>

## Sending it to the customer

Open the document on the web and use **Share link** → **Get link**. You get a link the customer can open without signing in, plus a **WhatsApp** button. See [Share links](/help/documents/share-links).

A proforma's link shows the **Amount due** and, if you have a UPI account set up, a UPI QR so the customer can pay the advance. A quotation's link shows only the total.

:::note
There is no PDF button on the Quotations or Proforma Invoices pages. The customer can download a PDF from the share link. That PDF currently uses the same layout and heading as a tax invoice, so mention in your message that it is a quotation. The shared web page itself is labelled correctly.
:::

## Converting to an invoice

When the customer confirms, convert the quotation or proforma instead of typing the invoice again.

<ForDesktop>
Hover over the row and click **Convert to Invoice**. You see **Converted to invoice**, and the app opens **Invoices**, where you will find the new draft invoice.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **To Invoice** on the card, then **Convert**. An alert shows the new invoice number. Open it from the **Invoices** tab.
</ForMobile>

The new invoice:

- is a **draft** sale invoice with the next invoice number;
- copies the customer, all line items, notes, terms, charges and round-off;
- keeps the quotation's **date and due date** — edit the draft if you want today's date;
- does not carry over a document-level discount;
- takes stock out when it is saved, like any sale invoice;
- links back to the quotation or proforma.

The quotation or proforma itself is **not changed**: its status stays the same and you can convert it again. See [Converting between documents](/help/documents).

<ForBusiness>
:::tip[Quote-to-cash in three steps]
1. Send the quotation with a share link on WhatsApp.
2. When the customer agrees, convert it and fix the invoice date if needed.
3. Mark the invoice as sent and share it. If you have a UPI account under **Cash & Bank**, the invoice link shows a UPI QR so the customer can pay straight away.
:::
</ForBusiness>

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a quotation reserve stock?**
No. Quotations and proformas never touch stock. Stock goes out only when you convert to an invoice (or send a delivery challan).

**Can I create a purchase quotation?**
Not in the app. The Quotations and Proforma Invoices pages only create sale documents.

**Why does the invoice made from my quotation have last month's date?**
Conversion keeps the quotation's date. Open the draft invoice, click **Edit**, and change the **Date** before you send it.

**How is a proforma different from an invoice in the books?**
A proforma is not an accounting entry. It does not add to the customer's ledger or to GSTR-1. Only the invoice you convert it into does.

**Can I edit a quotation after sending it?**
No — **Edit** is only offered on drafts. Create a new quotation with the changes.
