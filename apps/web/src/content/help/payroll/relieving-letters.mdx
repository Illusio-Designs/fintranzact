Open **Payroll → Full and final** and scroll to **Relieving letters**. A letter is available once an employee's exit is recorded.

## The wording

The letter text is yours. Fintranzact starts with standard wording; edit it and press **Save wording**. These placeholders are filled in for each employee:

`{{employee_name}}`, `{{employee_code}}`, `{{designation}}`, `{{department}}`, `{{date_of_joining}}`, `{{last_working_day}}`, `{{company_name}}` and `{{letter_date}}`.

A placeholder that is not on this list is refused when you save, and a value that is missing for an employee prints as a dash. You can also set the letter title, the place and the signatory's name and title.

## The letter

Press **Relieving letter** next to a name to download the PDF. It uses your business name, address and logo from your business settings, the employee's details, and your wording.

- The signature line is **blank**, for a person to sign. If you uploaded a signature image to your business, it is printed above the line.
- The letter is **not digitally signed**, and Fintranzact does not claim it is.
- Each letter you make is recorded in the audit log with who made it.

Have your lawyer or CA check the wording you use.
