**Ask Fintranzact AI** is a chat inside Fintranzact that answers questions about your business from your live data. It is a **paid add-on** (AI Assistant, with AI Plus for more questions). It is not part of your plan, and organisations in the Full Access Trial can try it with up to **50 questions**.

**The AI add-on is not on sale yet.** Until it is released, the Ask AI panel shows a notice that explains this and has no buy button. Organisations that already have it (for example from the trial, or granted by us) use everything described here.

## Open it

- Tap **Ask AI** in the top bar, on any page, or on the dashboard.
- On a computer it opens as a panel on the right. On a phone browser it fills the screen.
- Press **Esc** or the close button to put it away. Your focus goes back to where you were.

If you do not see the button, your role does not include the assistant (the auditor and filing-CA accountant roles do not have it).

## What you can ask

Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish. It answers in the language you asked in.

| You can ask | For example |
|---|---|
| Sales and profit | "How much did I sell this month?", "is mahine ki sales kitni hui?" |
| Dues | "How much do customers owe me?", "Which invoices are overdue?" |
| Top customers and items | "Who are my top 5 customers this year?" |
| Stock | "Which items are low on stock?" |
| Expiring batches | "Which batches expire in the next 30 days?" |
| GST | "What is my GST payable for last month?" |
| Cash and bank | "Cash aur bank mein kitna balance hai?" |
| Comparisons | "Compare this month's sales with last month" |
| Lookups | "Find invoice INV-12", "What does Asha Traders owe?" |
| Recent activity | "What was entered in the last 7 days?" |

The panel suggests a few questions to start with. Tap one to ask it.

## What you get back

An answer in text, saying which report and period it used. It may add **cards** under the answer: a small table, a simple bar chart, or a link that opens the invoice or report in the app. Links only ever go to pages inside Fintranzact.

Answers stream in as they are written. Press **Stop** to end one early.

## What it cannot do

- It only **reads** your books. It does not create, change, send or delete anything. To do that, use the normal screens.
- It never makes up numbers. If it cannot find something, it says so.
- It is not a tax or legal adviser. For filing and tax decisions, check with your CA. A GST payable figure comes from your books and may differ from a filed return.
- It does not answer questions that are not about your business.
- Payroll is not included.

## Your permissions still apply

The assistant reads your data **as you**, in the business you have open, with the permissions your role has. If you cannot open a report yourself, the assistant cannot read it for you: it will tell you that you do not have access to that information. It never shows another business's data.

## Questions and limits

- **AI Assistant** includes 150 questions a month and **AI Plus** includes 500. The count resets on the 1st of each month (Indian time).
- A Full Access Trial includes 50 questions in total.
- One question is one message that gets an answer. If the AI service has a problem and you get no answer, the question is not counted.
- Extra packs of 100 questions can be added to an organisation. The panel shows how many questions are left.
- When the questions run out, the panel says so. If extra packs are available to buy, the owner sees a **Buy more questions** button and everyone else is told to ask their owner.

## Plans, extra packs and how to buy

These are the prices and rules the AI add-on is planned to launch with. They apply only once the add-on is released; until then none of the buying options below appear.

| | AI Assistant | AI Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Questions included each month | 150 | 500 |
| Price per month, before GST | ₹399 | ₹999 |
| Billed yearly | Two months free (ten months' price) | Two months free (ten months' price) |

- **GST.** Prices are before 18% GST, which is added on the invoice, split into CGST and SGST or IGST by your billing state, exactly like your plan invoice. The invoice comes from Finvera Solutions LLP.
- **Extra packs.** A pack adds 100 questions for ₹199 before GST. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription. You can buy 1 to 50 packs in one go. Extra questions do not expire. They are used after your monthly included questions, and only while your organisation has an AI plan or a trial.
- **Who can buy.** Billing belongs to the organisation owner. Open **Settings, then Billing, then AI Assistant**. Choose a plan, or pick the number of packs and pay. A receipt is emailed and the GST invoice appears under **Invoices and payments**.
- **Changing plan.** Moving up from AI Assistant to AI Plus takes effect now, and the unused time on AI Assistant is credited. Moving down takes effect at the end of the period you paid for. You are never billed for both.
- **If a payment fails.** We keep retrying for a few days. After that the assistant is paused with a message that the add-on is inactive. Your chats are not deleted and you can still read them, and any extra questions you bought stay on your account until the AI plan is active again.
- **Refunds.** If a pack payment is refunded, the questions you have not used yet are taken back.

## Your chats

Each person has their own chat history. Open **Past chats** (the clock) to see yours, open one to continue it, or delete one. Nobody else in your organisation can see your chats, including the owner.

## Controls for the owner

Owners can open **Settings, then Team, then AI assistant** to switch the assistant off for the whole organisation, or for specific roles (admin, sales manager, salesperson, accountant). This is enforced by the server, not just hidden on screen. Owners always keep access so they can switch it back on.

Every question and each lookup the assistant makes is recorded in the activity log as **via AI assistant**. The log keeps a short copy of the question and the name of each lookup, not your business figures.

## Privacy

To answer, we send the AI provider your question and only the figures and names needed to answer it. We do not use your data to train AI models. Names and notes in your books are treated as data, never as instructions. Please do not type passwords, PAN, Aadhaar or bank account numbers into the chat: the assistant never asks for them and does not receive them from your books.
