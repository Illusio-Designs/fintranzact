A **credit note** reduces what a party owes — for example a price correction, a discount given after the sale, or a short supply you are not going to deliver. A **debit note** does the opposite and increases what is owed.

| | Credit Note | Debit Note |
|---|---|---|
| Default number | `CN-00001` | `DN-00001` |
| Statuses | Draft, Sent, Paid, Cancelled | Draft, Sent, Paid, Cancelled |
| Stock | No change | No change |
| Party ledger | Reduces the balance | Increases the balance (sales side) |
| GSTR-1 | **Credit Notes** section | **Debit Notes** section |
| Where to create | Web and mobile app | [CLI](/help/ai/cli) or [MCP server](/help/ai/mcp-server) only |

:::tip
If goods physically come back, use a [sales return](/help/documents/returns) instead. A credit note never puts stock back; a sales return does, and reduces the customer's balance in the same way.
:::

## Issuing a credit note against an invoice

This is the recommended way, because the credit note is linked to the invoice.

<ForDesktop>
1. Go to **Invoices** and click the invoice to open its detail panel.
2. Click **Issue Credit Note**. It is shown once the invoice is no longer a draft, is not cancelled, and has not already been fully credited.
3. The **New Credit Note** panel opens pre-filled with the invoice's party, line items, notes and terms, dated today.
4. Change the quantities or prices to the amount you are crediting, and remove lines you are not crediting.
5. Click **Create Credit Note**.

The invoice's detail panel now shows a **See CN-…** link to the credit note.

**Issue Credit Note** also appears on purchase invoices. There it creates a purchases-side credit note — a credit you received from the supplier.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
1. Open the invoice from the **Invoices** tab.
2. Tap **Issue Credit Note**. It is hidden for drafts, cancelled invoices and invoices that are already fully adjusted.
3. The **New Credit Note** screen opens pre-filled with the invoice's party and items, dated today.
4. Adjust the items under **Items (Returned)** and add a reason in **Notes (optional)**.
5. Tap **Create Credit Note**.
</ForMobile>

### What happens to the invoice

- The invoice's balance due goes down by the credit note's total. The shared invoice page shows it as **Credited**.
- Credit notes and sales returns against one invoice cannot add up to more than the invoice total. If they would, saving fails with *Amount exceeds remaining adjustable amount*, showing the invoice total, what is already adjusted, and what remains.
- When credit notes and returns together cover the whole invoice, the invoice's status shows as **Adjusted**, and **Issue Credit Note** / **Create Sales Return** disappear.
- A cancelled or deleted credit note no longer counts.

## Creating a standalone credit note

<ForDesktop>
Go to **VOUCHERS** → **Credit Notes**, choose **Sales** or **Purchases**, and click **+ New Credit Note**. Fill in the party, **Date**, line items, notes and terms, and click **Create Credit Note**.

A credit note made this way is **not linked** to any invoice — the form has no field to pick one. It still reduces the party's balance, but it does not reduce any particular invoice's balance, and GSTR-1 will not show an original invoice number for it.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **More** → **Credit notes**, then the **+** button. Tap **Select party...**, set the **Credit Note Date**, add items, and tap **Create Credit Note**. Credit notes created this way are sales-side and are not linked to an invoice.
</ForMobile>

:::caution
On the web form, the whole-document **Discount** box is not saved on credit notes, and **Round Off** only counts toward the saved total when the note also has a charge. Put any discount on the lines instead.
:::

## Managing credit notes

<ForDesktop>
The **Credit Notes** page has a **Sales** / **Purchases** switch and status tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Sent**, **Paid** and **Cancelled**. Columns are **Party**, **Credit Note #**, **Date**, **Ref. Invoice** (shows **Linked** when the note was issued from an invoice), **Status** and **Total**.

| Action | When it shows |
|---|---|
| **Mark Sent** | Draft |
| **Mark Paid** | Sent |
| **Delete** | Draft (Admin or Owner) |

Click a row to see the details. A linked note shows **Reference Invoice** with the invoice number — click it to jump to the invoice. Sales credit notes also have a **Share link** section, see [Share links](/help/documents/share-links). Drafts can be edited from the detail panel.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The **Credit notes** screen lists sales and purchase credit notes together, with chips **All**, **Draft**, **Sent**, **Paid** and **Cancelled**. Each card offers **Mark Sent** and **Delete** while it is a draft, and **Mark Paid** and **Cancel** once sent.

Tap a card to open it. You see the customer, **Credit Note Date**, **Source Invoice** (if linked), items and summary. Drafts have **Mark as Sent** and **Delete Credit Note**.
</ForMobile>

:::note
**Mark Paid** only changes the status label. It does not record a refund in **Cash & Bank** and does not change any balance — the credit note reduces the party's balance from the moment it is saved.
:::

## Effect on the party ledger

| Credit note on the… | Party ledger entry |
|---|---|
| **Sales** side (to a customer) | Credit — the customer owes you less |
| **Purchases** side (from a supplier) | Debit — you owe the supplier less |

See [Party Ledger](/help/parties/party-ledger).

## Effect on GST reports

- Every non-cancelled credit note dated in the month is listed in the **Credit Notes** section of [GSTR-1](/help/gst/gstr1), together with sales returns. If the note was issued from an invoice, the original invoice number is shown.
- In the portal GSTR-1 JSON, notes to customers with a GSTIN go to **CDNR**; notes to unregistered customers go to **CDNUR** when they amend a B2CL invoice, and are otherwise netted into the **B2CS** rows. See [GSTR-1 credit notes](/help/gst/gstr1#credit-notes).
- Sale debit notes are listed in the **Debit Notes** section and follow the same rules.

<ForCA>
:::caution[Check before filing]
- The **Credit Notes** section includes credit notes on **both** the sales and purchase side. Exclude supplier credit notes before reporting CDNR.
- The **Debit Notes** section includes purchase returns and purchase-side debit notes, which are inward documents and do not belong in GSTR-1.
- Reduce output tax in GSTR-3B by the eligible credit notes yourself — the 3B figures are not reduced automatically.
- GSTR-3B's ITC figure adds up the tax on **every** purchases-side document except orders and GRNs, so a supplier's credit note currently **adds** to ITC instead of reducing it. Correct ITC manually.
- Credit notes made from the **Credit Notes** page have no original invoice number; add it before filing.
- In the derived accounting (trial balance, P&L), every credit note is posted as **Dr Sales Returns + Output GST / Cr Receivable**, even when it is on the purchases side.
:::
</ForCA>

## Debit notes

Debit notes have no page in the web or mobile app. Create them with the [CLI](/help/ai/cli) or [MCP server](/help/ai/mcp-server). Once created, they appear in the party ledger and in GSTR-1.

| Debit note on the… | Party ledger | Derived accounting |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** side | Debit — the customer owes you more | Dr Receivable / Cr Sales + Output GST |
| **Purchases** side | Credit — you owe the supplier more | Dr Payable / Cr Purchase Returns + Input GST |

<ForCA>
:::note
For a purchase-side debit note, the party ledger and the derived accounting ledger move the supplier balance in opposite directions. If you issue debit notes to suppliers for returns, use a purchase return instead so both agree.
:::
</ForCA>

<ForBusiness>
:::tip[Which one should I use?]
- Customer paid too much or you gave a discount after billing → **Credit Note** from the invoice.
- Customer sent goods back → **Sales Return** from the invoice.
- You undercharged and need to bill the difference → create a new invoice for the difference (debit notes are not available in the app).
:::
</ForBusiness>

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is Issue Credit Note missing on my invoice?**
The invoice is still a draft, is cancelled, or is already fully covered by credit notes and returns (status **Adjusted**).

**Can I link an existing credit note to an invoice later?**
No. The link is set only when you create the credit note from the invoice. Delete the unlinked draft and issue a new one from the invoice.

**Does a credit note bring stock back?**
No. Use a [sales return](/help/documents/returns) when goods come back.

**Can I issue a credit note for more than the invoice amount?**
Not against an invoice. All credit notes and returns linked to one invoice together can't exceed its total.

**How do I refund the money?**
Fintranzact does not create a refund from a credit note. Record the money leaving your account under **Cash & Bank** (see [Banking](/help/banking)). Marking the credit note **Paid** only changes its label.
