With online payments, your customer opens an invoice, taps **Pay now**, and pays by UPI, card or net banking. The money goes **straight to your own Razorpay account and bank**. Fintranzact never holds it. When the payment succeeds, Razorpay tells Fintranzact and the invoice is marked paid (or part paid) automatically.

Online payments are set up on the web and desktop app, by an **owner or admin**. Your team can then send payment links from the invoice, and customers can pay from the shared invoice page.

## What you need

- A **Razorpay account** of your own, with **Payment Links** enabled.
- Your Razorpay **Key ID** and **Key secret**. In the Razorpay dashboard, open **Account & Settings**, then **API keys**, and generate a key. Use a `rzp_test_` key to try it without real money, then switch to a `rzp_live_` key.

Fintranzact's own Razorpay account is only used for Fintranzact subscription billing. It is never used to collect your customers' money.

## Connect your Razorpay account

1. Go to **Settings** and open the **Online payments** tab.
2. Enter the **Key ID** and **Key secret**.
3. Leave the **Webhook secret** blank for now and click **Connect Razorpay**.
4. Click **Test connection**. You should see *Connected. Razorpay accepted your keys.*

Your keys are encrypted when saved and are never shown again. The page only shows a masked Key ID such as `rzp_live_••••AbCd`, and whether you are in test or live mode.

## Add the webhook in your Razorpay dashboard

The webhook is how Razorpay tells Fintranzact that a customer paid. Without it, links still work but payments are **not** recorded automatically.

1. After connecting, the **Online payments** tab shows a **Webhook URL**. It is unique to your business. Click **Copy**.
2. In your Razorpay dashboard open **Settings**, then **Webhooks**, then **Add New Webhook**.
3. Paste the URL, and enter a **secret** of your own choosing.
4. Tick these events: `payment_link.paid`, `payment_link.partially_paid`, `payment_link.cancelled`, `payment_link.expired`, `payment.captured` and `payment.failed`.
5. Save the webhook in Razorpay.
6. Back in Fintranzact, click **Change keys**, enter the same secret in **Webhook secret**, and save. Leave the key fields as they were.

The webhook address contains a private token. Do not share it. If you change your keys later, the address stays the same, so you do not need to edit the webhook in Razorpay.

## Send a payment link from an invoice

1. Open an issued invoice from **Invoices**.
2. In the **Pay online** section click **Get payment link**. The link is copied.
3. Use **Copy**, **WhatsApp** or **Open** to send it to your customer.

The link always asks for the invoice's **balance due**, worked out by Fintranzact (the total, less payments received and credit notes or returns). Customers may pay part of it. If the balance changes later, for example after a part payment, the section offers a **New link** for the new balance and the old link is cancelled.

You cannot make a link for a draft, a cancelled invoice or an invoice that is already paid. The section only appears once Razorpay is connected.

## The customer's Pay now button

The shared invoice page (see [Share Links](/help/documents/share-links)) shows a **Pay now** button next to **Download PDF** while money is still due and Razorpay is connected. It opens a Razorpay payment page for the balance due. Afterwards the customer comes back to the invoice page.

## How payments are recorded

When Razorpay confirms a payment, Fintranzact:

- Records a payment against the invoice for the **amount actually paid**, with Razorpay's payment ID as the reference, and a payment mode of **UPI**, **Credit card**, **Debit card**, **Net banking**, **Wallet** or **Other**.
- Updates the invoice to **Paid**, or **Partial** if only part was paid.
- Does this **once per Razorpay payment**. If Razorpay sends the same event twice, nothing is duplicated.
- If a customer pays more than the balance, the whole payment is recorded, the invoice is paid, and the extra stays on the customer's account as an advance.

If an invoice was cancelled, deleted, or falls in a locked period, the payment is not booked automatically. Fintranzact leaves a note in the **Audit log** so you can record it by hand.

### Gateway charges

Razorpay deducts its fee before settling to your bank. To track it, create a **payment gateway** account named **Razorpay** under **Cash & Bank** (see [Payment Gateways](/help/banking/payment-gateways)). Online payments are then booked to that account, the charge is recorded as an expense, and the net amount moves to your settlement account.

The charge uses the fee Razorpay reports for the payment. If Razorpay does not report one, the rates you set on the gateway account are used. Without a Razorpay gateway account, the payment is recorded on its own, with no charge.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on the **Online payments** tab to delete your keys. Open payment links are cancelled and payments already recorded stay as they are.

## If your plan is read-only

If your organisation is read-only (for example the trial ended), you cannot connect keys or make new links, and the **Pay now** button is not shown. A payment a customer already made is still recorded when Razorpay reports it. **Test connection** and **Disconnect** keep working.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
|---|---|
| *Razorpay rejected these keys* | Copy the Key ID and Key secret again. Make sure they are both test keys or both live keys. |
| Invoice not marked paid after paying | Check the webhook is added in Razorpay with the exact URL, the six events, and that the same secret is saved in **Webhook secret**. |
| **Pay online** section missing | Razorpay is not connected, or the invoice is a draft, cancelled, paid, or has less than ₹1 due. |
| Payment link could not be created | Make sure **Payment Links** is enabled on your Razorpay account. |
