Both are on **Payroll → Filings and registers → Income tax on salary (TDS)**. They are built from your **approved** payroll runs and the TDS payments you recorded.

**Fintranzact files nothing with the Income Tax Department.** These are working documents for you or your CA.

## Form 24Q working data

Choose the financial year and the quarter (Q1 is April to June) and **Form 24Q data**. Two CSV files download:

- **Deductees**: for each employee and month, the section (192), PAN, the amount paid, the TDS deducted and the date of deduction.
- **Challans**: each TDS payment you recorded on the **Statutory dues** tab, with the challan number, date and amount.

It is **not an FVU file**. Your CA prepares the return in the Income Tax Department's return preparation utility, using this data, and files it. Check the layout and the data against what the department asks for.

## Form 16 working copy

Choose an employee and the financial year, then **Form 16 (PDF)** or **Form 16 data (CSV)**. The document has a **Part A style** summary (the tax deducted, quarter by quarter) and a **Part B style** computation: gross salary, the standard deduction, declared deductions, taxable income, tax, rebate, cess, the total tax payable, the tax deducted, and the difference (short or excess deducted). The year's tax is recomputed on the **actual** income of the months paid, with the same rules as the monthly projection.

Every copy is labelled **"Working copy for CA review"**. It is **not generated by TRACES** and is **not a validated certificate**. The official Form 16 is generated from the TRACES portal after the quarterly returns are filed and processed.

## Before you rely on it

The slabs, the rebate, the cess and the rounding come from your [Statutory settings](/help/payroll/statutory-settings). Surcharge and senior-citizen slabs are not computed. **Have your CA review** the figures.
