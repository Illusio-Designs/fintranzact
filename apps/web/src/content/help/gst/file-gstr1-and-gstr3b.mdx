You can file your monthly **GSTR-1** and **GSTR-3B** (and nil returns) without leaving Fintranzact. A guided window walks you through every step, and **nothing is filed until you confirm at the very end**. You can close the window at any point and come back later, even days later: Fintranzact remembers where you stopped.

:::note
Filing needs the GST portal connection (Sandbox) to be switched on for your account, the business GSTIN in **Settings**, and a role that may file: the owner, an admin or a filing accountant. A read-only accountant or auditor sees the **Return status** panel but no **File return** button.
:::

## Start

1. Click **GST** in the left navigation and open the **GSTR-1** or **GSTR-3B** tab.
2. Choose the **Month** and **Year** you want to file.
3. Click **File return**. If you started before, the button says **Resume filing**.

On a phone, the **GST returns** screen under **More** works the same way, one step per screen.

## The steps

### 1. Check

Fintranzact checks the GST portal for earlier returns. If an earlier month is not filed, or (for GSTR-3B) GSTR-1 of the same month is not filed, you are asked to **file those first** and a link takes you to them. If the portal cannot be reached you get a warning only, so make sure earlier returns are filed yourself. Fintranzact assumes you file monthly, because it does not record whether you file monthly or quarterly.

You also see a short summary of your books for the month (invoice count, taxable value, tax). For GSTR-3B, a note appears if its outward supplies differ from GSTR-1.

### 2. Sign in to the GST portal

Type your **GST portal username** and click **Send OTP**. The GST portal sends a one-time password to the mobile number registered for that login. Type it in and click **Verify and continue**. The session lasts about **6 hours**, and the window shows the time left. The OTP is never stored.

### 3. Prepare and save

For **GSTR-1** you choose a **Normal** or **Nil** return, and type two turnover figures. Fintranzact does not work these out for you:

- **Aggregate turnover of the previous financial year**: the total turnover of your whole last financial year (April to March). Your CA has this number. Enter 0 if you were not registered.
- **Aggregate turnover of the current financial year up to this period**: the total from 1 April up to the end of the month you are filing, including that month.

Click **Save to GST portal**. For **GSTR-3B**, you review the figures from your books and click **Save to GST portal**.

Then you see **Checking with the GST portal...**. The portal validates what you saved. This usually takes **1 to 2 minutes**, and Fintranzact checks every 12 seconds. If it takes longer than about three minutes, it stops and offers **Check again**. Nothing is lost.

### What "saved on the portal" means

After a successful save, your figures are **stored on the GST portal as a draft**. The return is **not filed**. You can still change it. Only the last step, with the filing OTP, files it.

### If the portal finds problems

The GST portal's own messages are listed by section (for example **B2B invoices** or **HSN summary**), with a **Fix in your books** link where the cause is usually found. Correct the books, then click **Save again**. You cannot go on until the portal accepts the save.

### 4. Review

- **GSTR-1**: click **Prepare for filing**, wait for the check, then **Load summary**. You see the portal's totals section by section. Check them against your books and click **The summary is correct: continue**.
- **GSTR-3B**: see the next section on set-off.

### 5. Send the OTP and file

Click **Send OTP**. The GST portal sends an EVC OTP to the mobile number and email registered for the **PAN holder** of the registration. If your PAN is different (for example a company's authorised signatory), use **Use a different PAN**. Type the 6 digits (pasting works). **Resend OTP** is available after 30 seconds.

Then comes the **final confirmation**: *File GSTR-1 for August 2026 for GSTIN ...*. Tick **I understand this files the return with the government and cannot be undone**. Only then does the **File** button work. If you spot a mistake after filing, you can only correct it with an amendment.

### 6. Done

You see the **ARN** (acknowledgement number) and the filing date. If the portal has not shown the ARN yet, click **Check again** after a few minutes. **Return status** on the GST page keeps showing it. To download the filed return, use your GST portal login.

## Nil returns

A nil return is offered only when **your books show nothing for that month**: no sales, credit notes or debit notes (GSTR-1), and no outward supplies, reverse charge, credit or tax (GSTR-3B). If your books have anything, the window tells you what and you file the normal return instead. A nil return needs an explicit tick: *I confirm there were no outward supplies in August 2026* (GSTR-1) or *no transactions* (GSTR-3B). Exports, advances and interest are not visible to Fintranzact, so check that none apply.

## GSTR-3B and set-off, in plain words

Your tax for the month is paid in two ways: from your **input tax credit** (the GST you paid on purchases) and, for the rest, in **cash**. Fintranzact reads both balances from the GST portal and **proposes** a split. It shows:

- the balances in your **credit ledger** and **cash ledger**;
- which credit pays which tax: IGST credit pays IGST, then CGST, then SGST; CGST credit pays CGST, then IGST (never SGST); SGST credit pays SGST, then IGST (never CGST);
- the **cash payable** for each tax. Cash of one tax cannot pay another. Tax on reverse charge is always paid in cash.

Nothing is paid yet. Read it, tick **I have reviewed this set-off**, and click **Confirm set-off**. If the proposal changed in the meantime, Fintranzact asks you to check the balances again and confirm the new one.

If your **cash ledger is short**, the window shows how much to add for each tax. **Depositing cash (a challan) is done on the GST portal: Fintranzact cannot make the payment.** After you deposit it, click **Check balances again**.

<ForCA>
The proposal covers IGST, CGST and SGST only. Cess, interest, late fee, blocked credit and ITC reversals are not modelled: add interest and late fee on the portal if they apply. ## If your session expires

The GST portal session lasts about 6 hours. If it ends while you work, the window takes you back to **Sign in to the GST portal** with a message. Request a new OTP, and you continue exactly where you stopped. Your progress is kept on the server, so closing the browser or coming back the next day works the same way.

## Common questions

**Did it file? I am not sure.** Open **Return status** on the GST page and click **Refresh**. If the portal shows an ARN, it is filed. If a filing attempt had a network error, check there before you try again.

**The OTP was rejected.** Nothing was filed. Click **Send a new OTP** and enter the new code.

**The portal says an earlier return is missing.** File that month first. The check window links to it.

**Can I file for a composition business?** Composition dealers file CMP-08 and GSTR-4. See [GSTR-4 and CMP-08 for composition dealers](/help/gst/gstr4-and-cmp08).
