<ForMobile>

Your employer invites you by email. Open the link, create a login with **that same email address**, and install or open the FinTranzact app. You will see four tabs: **Home**, **Attendance**, **Payslips** and **Leave**. You only ever see your own records.

## Check in and out

1. On **Home**, read what is collected and choose **I understand and agree**. This is asked once, and again if the wording changes.
2. Choose **Check in**. Allow the camera and location when your phone asks. Take a selfie.
3. You see "You are checked in." At the end of the day choose **Check out**.

The location is read **once** when you check in or out. The app never tracks you in the background. If you refuse the camera or the location, the check-in cannot be completed when your employer requires them. If you are outside your workplace you may see a message that your punch was saved for HR to review, or that it was refused, depending on your employer's rule. The time recorded is the server's time, so changing your phone clock does not change it.

## Attendance, payslips, Form 16 and leave

- **Attendance** shows each day of the month with your first check-in and last check-out, and a list of your check-ins with any flags such as "Late" or "Missing check-out".
- **Payslips** lists your approved payslips. Choose one to open or share the PDF. **Form 16** appears once your employer has released it. It is a **working copy prepared from your payslips**, not a certificate from the tax portal.
- **Leave** shows your balances. Choose **Apply for leave**, pick the type and dates, and send the request. You can cancel a request that is still waiting. You are emailed when it is decided.

To learn what is collected and for how long, see [Attendance photos and location: privacy](/help/payroll/attendance-privacy).

<ForDesktop>

Your employer invites you by email. Open the link, create a login with **that same email address**, and you will see your own screens: **Check in**, **Attendance**, **Payslips** and **Leave**. You cannot see anything else in the organization.

## Check in and out

1. Read what is collected and choose **I understand and agree** (asked once, and again if the wording changes).
2. Choose **Check in**, allow the camera and location in your browser, and take a selfie.
3. At the end of the day choose **Check out**.

A phone is the easiest way to check in. The location is read once per check-in, never in the background. The time recorded is always the server's time.

## Attendance, payslips, Form 16 and leave

- **Attendance** shows each day of the month with your check-in and check-out times and your check-ins with their flags.
- **Payslips** lists your approved payslips as PDFs. **Form 16** appears once your employer has released it, as a working copy prepared from your payslips.
- **Leave** shows your balances. Apply with the leave type and dates, and cancel a request that is still waiting. You are emailed when it is decided.

For what is collected and for how long, see [Attendance photos and location: privacy](/help/payroll/attendance-privacy).

</ForDesktop>
