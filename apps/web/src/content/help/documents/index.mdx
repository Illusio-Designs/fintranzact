Besides tax invoices, Fintranzact keeps several other business documents. They all share the same line-item form as an invoice (party, date, items, tax, discount, charges, notes and terms), but each one has a different effect on your stock, on what a party owes you, and on your GST returns.

Sales orders, purchase orders and goods receipt notes (GRNs) are covered in [Orders & GRN](/help/inventory/orders-and-grn).

## Document types at a glance

| Document | Use it for | Changes stock? | Changes party balance / ledger? | In GST returns? | Default prefix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quotation** | A price quote before the customer agrees | No | No | No | `QTN` |
| **Proforma Invoice** | A pre-invoice, usually to ask for an advance | No | No | No | `PI` |
| **Delivery Challan** | Goods sent out without a tax invoice yet | Yes — stock goes **out** | No | No | `DC` |
| **Credit Note** | Reducing what a party owes (price correction, discount after sale) | No | Yes — **reduces** the balance | GSTR-1 **Credit Notes** section | `CN` |
| **Debit Note** | Increasing what a party owes | No | Yes — **increases** the balance | GSTR-1 **Debit Notes** section | `DN` |
| **Sales Return** | A customer sends goods back | Yes — stock comes **in** | Yes — **reduces** the balance | GSTR-1 **Credit Notes** section | `SR` |
| **Purchase Return** | You send goods back to a supplier | Yes — stock goes **out** | Yes — **reduces** the balance | GSTR-1 **Debit Notes** section | `PR` |

Numbers run in their own sequence per document type, for example `QTN-00001`, `QTN-00002`. You can change the prefixes and next numbers in [Settings](/help/settings).

"Changes party balance / ledger" refers to the [party ledger](/help/parties/party-ledger) and statement, which only count invoices, credit notes, debit notes, sales returns and purchase returns. Cancelled documents never count.

:::caution
The **balance** figure shown next to each party in the **Parties** list currently also adds up open quotations, proformas and delivery challans for that party. If a customer's balance looks higher than their ledger, check for open quotations or challans. The party ledger itself is correct.
:::

:::note
**Debit Notes** and **Purchase Returns** have no screen of their own in the web or mobile app. You can create them through the [CLI](/help/ai/cli) or the [MCP server](/help/ai/mcp-server), and once created they show up in the party ledger and GST reports like any other document.
:::

### Where to find them

<ForDesktop>
All of these are in the **VOUCHERS** group of the left sidebar: **Quotations**, **Proforma Invoices**, **Delivery Challans**, **Sales Returns** and **Credit Notes**. Each page lists the documents of that type, with status tabs across the top, a search box, and a **+ New …** button.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **More** in the bottom tab bar. Under **SALES** you will find **Quotations**, **Proforma invoices**, **Delivery challans**, **Credit notes** and **Sales returns**. Each screen has a search bar, status chips, and a floating **+** button to create a new document.
</ForMobile>

### Statuses

Every new document is saved as **Draft**. Which statuses a document can move to depends on its type:

| Document | Statuses |
|---|---|
| Quotation, Proforma Invoice, Delivery Challan, Sales Return, Purchase Return | Draft, Sent, Cancelled |
| Credit Note, Debit Note | Draft, Sent, Paid, Cancelled |

- **Mark Sent** is available on drafts. It only changes the label.
- **Cancel** is available on sent documents in the mobile app. The web app has no cancel button on these pages.
- Cancelling a document that moved stock puts the stock back.
- In the app, only drafts can be edited (web only) or deleted. Deleting needs the **Admin** or **Owner** role. A delete is a soft delete: the document is cancelled and hidden from the list, and any stock it moved is put back.

## Converting between documents

A conversion creates a **new** document from an existing one. The new document is saved as a draft, gets the next number in its own sequence, and keeps a link to the source. On the web, the new document's detail panel shows **Made from …** or **Reference Invoice** with a clickable number.

| From | To | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Quotation | Invoice | **Convert to Invoice** on the Quotations list (web), **To Invoice** on the quotation card (mobile) |
| Proforma Invoice | Invoice | **Convert to Invoice** on the Proforma Invoices list (web), **To Invoice** on the card (mobile) |
| Delivery Challan | Invoice | **Convert** on the challan (web), **Convert to Invoice** on the challan's detail screen (mobile) |
| Invoice | Credit Note | **Issue Credit Note** on the invoice detail |
| Invoice | Sales Return | **Create Sales Return** on the invoice detail |
| Sales / purchase order, GRN | Challan, GRN or invoice | See [Orders & GRN](/help/inventory/orders-and-grn) |

### What gets copied

When you convert a quotation, proforma or challan:

- **Copied:** party, sale/purchase side, every line item (item, name, line notes, quantity, unit, price, tax %, line discount %), notes, terms and conditions, other charges and round-off.
- **Date:** the new invoice keeps the **source document's date and due date**. It is not re-dated to today. Edit the draft invoice if you need today's date.
- **Invoice-level discount** is not carried over.
- **Challans only:** only quantities still **not billed** can be copied (on the web you choose how much of each line to bill), and the invoice is saved **without moving stock again** — the challan already took the goods out. If you bill only part of a challan, its other charges and round-off are not copied.

When you issue a credit note or sales return from an invoice, the form opens pre-filled with the invoice's party, lines, notes, terms and round-off, dated today. You can change any of it before saving.

### Is the source marked as converted?

| Source | What happens to it |
|---|---|
| Quotation, Proforma | Nothing. Its status stays as it was, and the convert button stays visible. Converting it again makes a **second** invoice. |
| Delivery Challan | Its **Pending** column changes to **Partly billed** or **Billed**. Once fully billed, the **Convert** button disappears. |
| Invoice (credit note / return) | When credit notes and returns together cover the invoice total, the invoice shows as **Adjusted** and the create buttons disappear. |

:::caution
Because quotations and proformas are not marked as converted, keep track yourself — for example, cancel the quotation from the mobile app once it has been billed. The web **Convert to Invoice** button is shown on every row, including cancelled ones.
:::

<ForCA>
:::tip[For CAs]
Only tax invoices, credit notes, debit notes, sales returns and purchase returns reach the GST reports. Quotations, proformas and challans never do. When reconciling a client's books, check the **Reference** link on credit notes: GSTR-1 shows the original invoice number only when the note was issued from the invoice.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**Why don't I see a Debit Notes or Purchase Returns page?**
The app has no screen for them yet. Create them from the [CLI](/help/ai/cli) or [MCP server](/help/ai/mcp-server). They still appear in the party ledger and GST reports.

**Can I convert a quotation into a proforma or a delivery challan in the app?**
No. The app only offers quotation → invoice and proforma → invoice. Other conversions are only available through the API.

**I converted a quotation twice by mistake. What do I do?**
Delete the extra draft invoice from **Invoices**. The quotation itself was not changed by either conversion.

**Does a delivery challan affect what the customer owes?**
Not in the party ledger. The amount becomes due only when you convert the challan into an invoice.

**Can I edit a document after marking it Sent?**
No. The **Edit** button appears only on drafts. Cancel the document and create a new one instead.
