Plans in Fintranzact belong to your **organization** (the account that holds all your businesses and team members), not to an individual business. Every business and team member in the organization shares the organization's plan.

There is no free plan. Every new organization starts with a **Full Access Trial** (14 days, or 30 days through a partner referral) with no card needed. During it you get Business-level access and every add-on, with caps. See [Your free trial](/help/settings/free-trial) for the details.

## Available plans

Prices are before 18% GST, which is added at checkout. Pay yearly and you get two months free.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | ₹299 | ₹2,990 | One business and up to 3 users. Invoices, quotations, payments, parties and items, GST reports, e-way bills, basic inventory, recurring invoices and POS. |
| **Growth** (most popular) | ₹699 | ₹6,990 | Up to 3 businesses and 10 users. Everything in Starter, plus e-invoicing, multiple warehouses, batches and expiry, bank reconciliation, a basic online store, API access and data export. |
| **Business** | ₹1,499 | ₹14,990 | Unlimited businesses and users. Everything in Growth, plus manufacturing and bill of materials, full audit history, priority support and onboarding help. |

The full comparison, including the exact limits each plan enforces, is on the public [pricing page](/pricing). The numbers on that page are read from the same plan definitions the server uses to enforce limits. The platform team can adjust prices and limits, so the pricing page is always the current source.

Documents carry a small "Powered by Fintranzact" line at the end.

## What each plan includes

No plan limits how many invoices, quotations, payments, parties, items or other records you can create. Plans differ in the number of businesses and users and in which features they include.

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoices, quotations, payments, parties, items | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| GST reports and e-way bills | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Basic inventory (one default warehouse, stock adjustments), recurring invoices, POS | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| E-invoicing | No | Yes | Yes |
| Multiple warehouses and stock transfers | No | Yes | Yes |
| Batches and expiry | No | Yes | Yes |
| Bank reconciliation | No | Yes | Yes |
| Basic online store, API access, data export | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manufacturing and bill of materials | No | No | Yes |
| Full audit history, priority support, onboarding help | No | No | Yes |

The platform team can change which plan includes which feature, so the [pricing page](/pricing) is always the current source. During the free trial you can use the features of the top plan, and organizations that were on the earlier unlimited test plan keep every feature.

### When a feature is not on your plan

A feature your plan does not include stays visible: its menu item shows the plan that has it (for example **Growth**), and its page opens with a "Not on your plan" note and a **See plans** button. Create and save buttons for that feature are disabled and say why. The app also shows the same message if you try to use it from somewhere else, for example **E-invoicing is available on the Growth plan and above.**

Your existing data is never hidden. If you move to a smaller plan, everything you created with a feature (batches, extra warehouses, bank statements, bills of materials, e-way bills and so on) stays readable, searchable and printable. You cannot add to it or change it until you are on a plan that includes the feature. You can always stop or remove things: pause or delete a recurring invoice, remove a warehouse, or switch batch tracking off for an item.

If your organization is **read-only** (trial over or payment failed), the read-only message comes first: choose a plan to carry on.

## Choosing a plan after sign-up

When a new organization is created, Fintranzact shows the plan picker (**Choose your plan: Select the plan that fits your business**) before you can use the app. You also land on this screen right after you create a business.

1. Use the **Monthly / Yearly** switch to compare prices.
2. Click a plan card to see what it includes in the **Selected** panel on the right.
3. Click **Start 14-day free trial**. Your organization starts the trial on that plan, and you then create your company.

If you came from the pricing page, the plan you clicked there is already selected. You can switch plan during the trial. When you are ready to pay, open **Settings → Billing** and subscribe.

:::caution
If your organization already has a paid subscription and you see the plan picker again, changing the plan here is refused. Change it from **Settings → Billing** instead.
:::

## When your trial ends or a payment fails

If your trial ends, your plan ends, or a renewal payment fails and the grace period runs out, your organization becomes **read-only**. A banner at the top of every page says so. Nothing is deleted, and you can still view, search, download PDFs and export your data. Creating and editing is paused, and the app shows **Choose a plan** when you try.

- **Owners:** click **Choose a plan** (or open **Settings → Billing**) and subscribe. Access returns as soon as the payment goes through.
- **Everyone else:** the banner asks you to contact your organization owner, who is the only one who can pick a plan.
- **A renewal payment failed:** you get a few days of grace first, with a warning banner showing how long you have and an **Update payment** button.
- **Trial:** a banner counts the days down from the first day (for example **9 days left in your Full Access Trial**). You can dismiss it for the day until 3 days are left, when it turns amber and stays. You also get reminder emails at 7 days, 2 days and when the trial ends.
- **After the trial:** the banner says **Trial ended: read-only**. Choose any plan and everything unlocks straight away.

## Older organizations

Organizations that were on the earlier unlimited test plan keep full access: they were moved to Business with no trial and no payment, and they are never made read-only. This is not offered to new organizations.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a credit card to sign up?**
No. You sign up with your email or phone number and start the 14-day free trial. No payment details are collected until you subscribe.

**Is there a free plan?**
No. There are three paid plans. The free trial lets you use the product before you pay.

**How do I subscribe?**
Open **Settings → Billing**, pick a plan and a billing period, and pay. You don't need to re-enter any data.

**Does each of my businesses need its own plan?**
No. The plan is set on the organization, so every business inside it is covered, up to the plan's business limit.

<ForCA>
**I manage many client businesses. Which plan do I need?**
Starter covers one business, Growth up to three and Business has no limit on businesses or users. If you look after many clients, Business is the plan that fits.
