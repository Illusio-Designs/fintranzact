Open **Payroll → Payroll runs** and choose **+ Start payroll** for a month. There is one run per month for each business. A run moves through these steps:

| Step | Status | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Lock attendance** | Attendance locked | The month's attendance is frozen. Every working day needs a mark; if some are missing you choose to count them as present or absent, or go back and mark them |
| 2. **Calculate payroll** | Calculated | Each employee's pay is worked out: earnings, less loss of pay, less deductions, equals net pay |
| 3. **Submit for approval** | Pending approval | Sends the figures for checking |
| 4. **Approve** | Approved | A second person approves. Payslips are created and everything is frozen |
| 5. **Post to books** | Posted | One journal entry records the salary expense and what you owe |
| 6. **Mark as paid** | Paid | Records the payment from a bank or cash account |

You can **recalculate** as often as you like before approval, and **reopen** the run to a draft (the calculated figures are discarded and attendance can be changed again). **An approved run cannot be changed or reopened.**

## What calculation does

For each employee the run takes the salary in force for the month, the paid days from attendance, overtime, any leave encashment waiting for payment and the adjustments you add, and works out gross earnings, deductions and net pay. Each amount is rounded to the paisa once; totals are the plain sums, so a payslip always adds up. A line shows the paid days, loss of pay days, and expands to show every component.

**Things to check** appear above the lines, for example an employee left out because they have no salary yet, or wages below 50 percent of the remuneration. Warnings never stop you; a **negative net pay** does stop you from submitting until you reduce a deduction.

### Adjustments and statutory deductions

Use **Adjust** on an employee to add a one-off **deduction** (advance recovery, loan instalment, fine) or **earning** (incentive, arrears). Adjustments are kept when you recalculate and shown on the payslip.

If the business is registered for them in [Statutory settings](/help/payroll/statutory-settings), **provident fund, ESI, professional tax, labour welfare fund and income-tax TDS are calculated automatically** and appear as their own columns and lines (employee shares as deductions, employer shares listed separately). Do not also enter them as adjustments: the run warns when a manual deduction looks like a statutory one. Missing slabs (for example a state's professional tax slabs) deduct nothing and show up in **things to check**.

## Approval needs a second person

The person who last **calculated** the run cannot approve it. Another **owner or admin** approves. A business with only one user can approve its own payroll, since nobody else can check it. Accountants prepare payroll but cannot approve it.

If you calculated the run yourself, the run page tells you why **Approve** is not offered. Recalculating makes you the person who calculated it, so a different person should recalculate or approve.

## Posting to your books

**Post to books** writes one journal entry dated the last day of the month, and shows it in **Journal entries**:

| Debit | Credit |
|---|---|
| Salary & Wages (Basic, DA, retaining allowance) | Salaries Payable (the net pay) |
| Salary - Allowances (HRA, conveyance, special and other earnings) | Payroll Deductions Payable (what was held back from employees) |
| Salary - Bonus & Incentives | Employer Contributions Payable |
| Salary - Overtime | |
| Employer Contributions to Staff | PF and EPS Payable, ESI Payable, Professional Tax Payable, Labour Welfare Fund Payable, TDS on Salary Payable (each only if the run has that statutory amount, employee and employer shares together) |

The entry always balances. The accounts are created in your chart of accounts the first time you post. Posting twice does nothing the second time. If the month is inside a locked period, posting is refused until the owner unlocks it (see [Period locks](/help/accounting/period-locks)).

**Mark as paid** records the salaries going out of the bank or cash account you choose: Salaries Payable is cleared against the bank, and the account balance goes down by the net pay. Do the bank transfers first (the [bank payment file](/help/payroll/payslips) helps), then mark the run paid. The statutory amounts stay as liabilities until you pay them to the authority: record each payment on the **Statutory dues** tab (see [Statutory files and registers](/help/payroll/statutory-files-and-registers)).

A payroll entry cannot be voided from Journal entries, because it belongs to an approved run.

## If something was wrong

An approved run is final. Correct the next month's run with an adjustment, and have your accountant make any correcting journal entry. Check the structure and the figures with your CA before you rely on payroll.
