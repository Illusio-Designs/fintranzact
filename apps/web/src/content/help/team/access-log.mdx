Your CA works in your books with their own login, and everything that matters is recorded. Owners and admins can see it; nobody else can.

## The access log

<ForDesktop>
Go to **Settings → Team**. Under the members table is the **Access log**. Use the tabs to show only **Invites**, **Role changes**, **Removals**, **Opened** or **Downloads**, and click **Load more** for older events.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Open **More → Settings → Team**. The access log is under the list of members and shows the latest 25 events.
</ForMobile>

It shows, with who did it and when:

- who was invited, and who withdrew an invitation
- who accepted
- when someone's access level was changed
- when access was removed, or when your CA left on their own
- when your CA **opened your books** (at most once an hour, so a busy day is a handful of lines, not hundreds)
- which reports and files your CA **downloaded**, such as the GSTR-1 JSON, a party ledger CSV, a Tally export or a TDS certificate

In the members list, each CA shows **Last opened** with how long ago they were last in your books, or **Never opened**.

The access log is kept without a time limit, whatever your plan.

## What your CA changed or filed

When your CA with filing access saves or files a GST return, uploads or matches GSTR-2B, or marks a month as filed, it appears in the **activity log** under **Settings → Account**, with their name and access level (for example "Accountant (filing)"). Passcodes (OTPs) and signatory details are never written there. The activity log follows your plan's history window.

## What is not recorded

Looking at invoices, reports or screens is not logged. Downloads by you and your staff are not listed here either; the access log is about your accountant.

## For developers and scripts

- CLI: `fintranzact tenant access-log`
- MCP: `tenant_access_log`
- API: `tenant.accessLog`
