A share link is a web address for one document that your customer can open on any phone or computer **without signing in**. From it they can read the document, pay by UPI, and download a PDF.

Share links are created and managed in the **web and desktop app**. The mobile app cannot create or turn off links, but links made on the web open fine on a customer's phone.

## Which documents can be shared

Any **sales-side** document that has not been deleted:

- Invoices
- Quotations and proforma invoices
- Delivery challans (Sales)
- Credit notes (Sales) and sales returns
- Sales orders

Purchase-side documents (purchase invoices, purchase credit notes, purchase challans) have no share link.

## Creating a link

1. Open the document's detail panel — click the row on **Invoices**, **Quotations**, **Proforma Invoices**, **Delivery Challans**, **Credit Notes** or **Sales Returns**.
2. Find the **Share link** section. It reads *Anyone with the link can view it, pay and download the PDF — no sign-in.*
3. Click **Get link**. The link is created and copied to your clipboard (**Link copied**).

Once a link exists, the section shows:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Link box | The full link, selected when you click it |
| **Copy** | Copies the link again |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens WhatsApp with a ready message such as `Invoice INV-00012: <link>`, addressed to the party's phone number. 10-digit numbers get the `91` country code added. If the party has no usable phone number, WhatsApp opens so you can pick the chat. |
| **Not opened yet** / **Opened 3 times, last on …** | How often the link was opened, and when last |
| **Turn off link** | Stops the link working (see below) |

Each document has **one** live link. Clicking **Get link** again, or opening the panel on another computer, shows the same link.

:::note
Making a link needs a role that can create documents (not **Viewer**). Anyone who can see the document can see and copy an existing link.
:::

## What the customer sees

The page at `/i/…` shows:

- **Your business** — logo, name, address, GSTIN, phone and email.
- **The document** — its type and number (for example *Quotation QTN-00004*), date, and for payable documents the due date.
- **The headline amount** — **Amount due** while something is still owed, **Paid** once settled, or **Total** for documents that are not asked to be paid. Cancelled documents are marked **Cancelled**.
- **Bill to** — the party's name, address and GSTIN.
- **Items** — item, HSN and line notes, quantity and unit, rate, tax % and amount.
- **Totals** — Subtotal, Discount, Tax, Other charges, Round off and Total. For payable documents with payments or credits, also **Credited**, **Paid** and **Balance**.
- **Notes** and **Terms**.
- **Download PDF** — an A4 PDF of the document.
- On a plan that shows Fintranzact branding, a small **Made with Fintranzact** link at the bottom.

### Paying from the link

Invoices, proforma invoices and debit notes that still have an amount due also show payment options:

| Option | Shown when | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay with UPI** button | On phones | Opens the customer's UPI app with your UPI ID, the amount and the document number filled in |
| **Scan to pay with any UPI app** QR | Always | For paying from another phone; shows your UPI ID and the amount |
| **Bank transfer** | You have a savings or current account | Account name, account number, IFSC and bank |

UPI options appear only if you have a **UPI** account under **Cash & Bank** — its account number is used as the UPI ID. See [Banking](/help/banking).

Quotations, delivery challans, credit notes and sales returns show only their total, with no payment options.

:::caution
- A payment made through the link is **not recorded automatically**. When the money arrives, record it under **Payments** as usual.
- If an invoice has credit notes or returns against it, the page shows the reduced **Balance**, but the UPI QR and **Pay with UPI** button ask for the total minus payments only, **without** the credits. Tell the customer the correct amount, or collect the balance another way.
- The downloaded PDF uses the tax-invoice layout and heading for every document type, including quotations and challans. The web page itself is labelled correctly.
:::

## Turning a link off

1. Open the document and click **Turn off link** in the **Share link** section.
2. Confirm **Turn off** in the **Turn off this link?** dialog.

The old link stops working immediately. Anyone who opens it sees **This link is not valid any more** — *Ask the business that sent it for a new link.* To share the document again, click **Get link**; you get a **new** link, and the old one stays dead.

## How long a link lasts

Links **do not expire**. A link keeps working until:

- you turn it off,
- the document is deleted, or
- your organisation is suspended.

The page always shows the document's **current** state — if you record a payment, the amount due updates; if you cancel the document, the customer sees it as cancelled.

Share pages are marked so search engines do not index them, and they are not cached. Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with the customer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I set an expiry date on a link?**
No. Links last until you turn them off or delete the document.

**Does editing the document change the link?**
No. The link always shows the latest version of the document.

**I turned a link off by mistake. Can I get the same link back?**
No. Click **Get link** to make a new one and send that instead.

**Why is there no Share link on my purchase invoice?**
Only sales-side documents can be shared, because they are the ones you send out.

**Can I create a share link from the mobile app?**
Not yet. Create it on the web or desktop app; the customer can open it on any device.
