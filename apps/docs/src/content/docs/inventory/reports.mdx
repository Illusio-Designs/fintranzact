All inventory reports are on the **Reports** page (sidebar **Reports**), in the **Inventory** and **Orders** groups. Every report has an **Export CSV** button.

The date range bar at the top is shared by all reports, but not every inventory report uses it:

| Uses | Reports |
|---|---|
| The full date range | Stock Ledger, Movement Summary |
| Only the end date ("as on") | Stock Group Summary, Price List |
| Today's stock (date range ignored) | Stock Summary, Godown Summary, Stock Ageing, Reorder Status, Dead Stock, all pending order reports |

Stock values follow your [valuation method](/inventory/stock-valuation) (Average cost or FIFO).

<ForBusiness>
A good weekly routine: **Reorder Status** to decide what to buy, **Pending Sales Orders** to see who's waiting, and once a month **Dead Stock** to find money stuck on your shelves.
</ForBusiness>

## Stock Summary

Current stock of every product.

- **Cards:** Total Cost Value, Total Sale Value (stock × sale price), SKU Count, Low Stock (items at or below their alert level).
- **Filters:** a stock group (includes its sub-groups) and **Show Zero Stock**.
- **Columns:** Item (with a **Low** badge), Category, HSN, Unit, Stock, Purchase Price, Sale Price, Stock Value.
- Items with variants are a row you can expand to see each variant.

Stock Value uses the valuation method. The Purchase Price column shows the price from the item form, for reference.

## Stock Ledger

Every movement of one item, with a running balance. Like Tally's stock item vouchers.

1. Choose an **Item** (and a **Variant**, for items with variants).
2. Optionally pick a warehouse (shown when you have two or more), or keep **All warehouses**.
3. Set the date range.

- **Cards:** Opening, Inward, Outward, Closing.
- **Columns:** Date, Particulars, Warehouse, Inward, Outward, Balance.
- **Particulars** says what caused each movement, for example "Sale INV-104", a delivery challan or goods receipt note, "Transfer in", "Transfer out", an adjustment with its reason, a physical stock count, opening stock, or a manufacturing journal. The party name is shown underneath.

Up to 2,000 movements are shown. Narrow the date range to see the rest. The CSV includes opening and closing rows.

## Movement Summary

Opening, inward, outward and closing stock for every item over the date range.

- **Filter:** warehouse.
- **Columns:** Item, Opening, Inward, Outward, Closing, Value (closing value). The total value is shown at the top.
- Items with no movement and no closing stock are hidden.
- With **All warehouses**, transfers between your own warehouses are left out, since they don't change your total.

## Godown Summary

Stock held and its value in each warehouse, today.

- **Columns:** Warehouse (with code and premises; inactive warehouses greyed), Items, Quantity, Value, and a total value.
- [Unplaced stock](/inventory/warehouses#unplaced-stock) is counted in your sales default warehouse.

## Stock Ageing

How long your current stock has been sitting.

- **Buckets:** 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 91–180 days, Over 180 days. Each bucket has a value card; the two oldest are highlighted in amber.
- The table shows the quantity in each bucket per item, oldest stock first. The CSV shows values.
- Stock is aged first in, first out: what you still hold is taken to be what arrived most recently. Stock older than your recorded history falls into **Over 180 days**.

## Reorder Status

Items at or below their reorder level (the item's **Low Stock Alert**), with a suggested quantity to order.

- **Order for:** 15, 30 (default), 45, 60 or 90 days.
- **Suggested order** = the shortfall to the reorder level + that many days of sales at the last 30 days' pace, rounded up. Sales here means invoices and delivery challans, net of returns.
- **Columns:** Item (SKU), On hand, Reorder level, Sold / day, Days left, Suggested order.
- Only items with a Low Stock Alert set can appear. If nothing needs ordering, you see "Nothing to reorder".

## Dead Stock

Stock you hold that hasn't gone out in a while.

- **Not sold in:** 30, 60, 90 (default), 180 or 365 days.
- An item counts as sold if it went out on an invoice, delivery challan or purchase return in that time.
- **Columns:** Item, Category, Quantity, Value, Last sold ("Never" if it never has). The header shows how many items and how much value is stuck. Sorted by value, highest first.

## Stock Group Summary

Quantity and value per [stock group](/inventory/stock-groups), as on the end date of the range.

- Starts at **All groups**. Click a group to drill into its sub-groups, then its items. A **Not in a group** row collects ungrouped items.
- **Columns:** Particulars (with item count), Quantity, Rate (item rows), Value.
- A group's totals include all its sub-groups.

Quantities are added up across items even when units differ, so rely on the Value column for groups that mix units.

## Price List

Every item's price on each [price level](/inventory/price-levels), as on the end date of the range, for one unit.

- **Filter:** Category (type the exact category name).
- **Columns:** Item (with category), Unit, Sale price, MRP, then one column per price level. The default level is marked **(default)**.
- Where a level has no price for an item, the sale price is shown greyed. Level prices shown are for the first quantity slab.
- Items with variants show a row per variant. Services are included.

## Orders reports

Four reports in the **Orders** group show what's still pending, line by line:

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Pending Sales Orders** | Ordered by customers and not yet delivered |
| **Pending Purchase Orders** | Ordered from suppliers and not yet received |
| **Pending GRNs** | Goods received and not yet billed |
| **Pending Delivery Challans** | Goods delivered and not yet billed |

- **Filters:** a party. The order reports also have **Past delivery date only**.
- **Columns:** Date, number, Party, Item, Ordered, Delivered / Received / Billed, Pending, Value. The order reports add **Delivery by**, in red when it has passed.
- A summary line shows the number of documents and the pending value before tax (pending quantity × rate after line discount).
- Short-closed, cancelled and deleted documents are left out. Drafts are included. Oldest first.

See [Orders & GRN](/inventory/orders-and-grn).

## Item-wise Sales

Also in the Inventory group: sales quantity and revenue per item for the date range. See [Business Reports](/reports#item-wise-sales).

For a year-end stock statement, run **Stock Group Summary** with the date range ending on 31 March. It gives closing quantity and value by group, matching the closing stock used in the P&L and Balance Sheet for that date. **Movement Summary** for the full year gives the quantity reconciliation (opening + inward − outward = closing) per item.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why doesn't changing the date range change the Stock Summary?**
Stock Summary, Godown Summary, Stock Ageing, Reorder Status, Dead Stock and the pending order reports always show today's position. For stock on a past date, use Stock Group Summary (as on the end date) or Movement Summary.

**Why is an item missing from Reorder Status?**
Only items with a **Low Stock Alert** set, and at or below it, appear.

**Why doesn't Movement Summary show my transfers?**
With **All warehouses**, transfers are left out because they don't change your total stock. Pick a warehouse to see transfers in and out of it.

**Can I see stock by rack or bin?**
No. Reports work at warehouse level.
