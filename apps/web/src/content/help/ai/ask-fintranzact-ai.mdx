**Ask Fintranzact AI** is a chat inside Fintranzact that answers questions about your business from your live data. It is a **paid add-on** (AI Assistant, with AI Plus for more questions). It is not part of your plan, and organisations in the Full Access Trial can try it with up to **50 questions**.

**The AI add-on is not on sale yet.** Until it is released, the Ask AI panel shows a notice that explains this and has no buy button. Organisations that already have it (for example from the trial, or granted by us) use everything described here.

## Open it

- Tap **Ask AI** in the top bar, on any page, or on the dashboard.
- On a computer it opens as a panel on the right. On a phone browser it fills the screen.
- Press **Esc** or the close button to put it away. Your focus goes back to where you were.

If you do not see the button, your role does not include the assistant (the auditor and filing-CA accountant roles do not have it).

## What you can ask

Ask in English, Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish (Hindi in English letters). By default it answers in the language you asked in; you can choose the language instead, see Reply language below.

| You can ask | For example |
|---|---|
| Sales and profit | "How much did I sell this month?", "is mahine ki sales kitni hui?" |
| Dues | "How much do customers owe me?", "Which invoices are overdue?" |
| Top customers and items | "Who are my top 5 customers this year?" |
| Stock | "Which items are low on stock?" |
| Expiring batches | "Which batches expire in the next 30 days?" |
| GST | "What is my GST payable for last month?" |
| Cash and bank | "Cash aur bank mein kitna balance hai?" |
| Comparisons | "Compare this month's sales with last month" |
| Lookups | "Find invoice INV-12", "What does Asha Traders owe?" |
| Recent activity | "What was entered in the last 7 days?" |
| How to use Fintranzact | "How do I create an invoice?", "Where do I record a payment?" (answered from the help centre, with a link to the article) |

The panel suggests a few questions to start with, in your reply language. Tap one to ask it.

## Speak your question

If your browser supports it, a **microphone** button sits next to the question box.

1. Tap the microphone and allow microphone access if your browser asks.
2. Speak your question. The words appear in the box as you speak.
3. Tap the microphone again, or press **Esc**, to stop. It also stops by itself when you pause.
4. Read the text, fix anything it heard wrongly, and press **Send**.

Nothing is sent until you press Send. A question you speak counts as one question, exactly like one you type.

- The microphone listens for your reply language: English and Hinglish are heard as Indian English, Hindi as Hindi, Gujarati as Gujarati. Change it under Reply language below.
- If the microphone button is missing, your browser has no speech recognition (Chrome, Edge and Safari have it; Firefox does not). Type your question instead.
- If it says the microphone is blocked, allow microphone access for this site in your browser's settings and try again. If it says it could not hear you, check your microphone or move closer. If it asks for an internet connection, your browser's speech service could not be reached.
- Voice input is on the web app only for now. The mobile app does not have the assistant yet.

**Privacy of your voice.** Voice input uses your browser's own speech recognition. Fintranzact does not receive, record or keep your audio. Depending on your browser, the browser itself may send what you say to its maker's speech service (for example Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, Apple for Safari) to turn it into text, under that company's terms. Only the text that ends up in the box is sent to Fintranzact, when you press Send.

## Reply language

Open **Preferences** (the gear button at the top of the panel) and choose **Reply language**:

| Choice | The assistant replies in |
|---|---|
| Auto | The language of your question: English, Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish |
| English | English |
| Hindi | Hindi, in Devanagari |
| Gujarati | Gujarati, in Gujarati script |
| Hinglish | Hindi written in English letters |

Amounts always use the ₹ sign and lakh and crore grouping, for example ₹12,34,567.50. Amounts, dates, names and invoice numbers are copied exactly from your books and are never translated. Terms such as GST, GSTIN, HSN and e-way bill stay as they are. The setting belongs to you and does not change anyone else's.

The assistant is an AI model, so its Hindi and Gujarati can still contain mistakes. Check important figures against the report it names.

## Tips on your dashboard

The dashboard can show a **Tips from your assistant** card: a few short, useful things worked out from your books, without you asking.

- **Overdue invoices**: how many, how much is owed, and how long the oldest has been overdue.
- **Low stock**: how many items are at or below their reorder level.
- **Expiring batches**: batches that have expired or will expire in the next 30 days.
- **GST due dates**: GSTR-1 (11th) and GSTR-3B (20th) for the month just ended, in the week before they are due, if you are registered for regular GST and had sales that month. Due dates are often extended, so check with your CA.

Each tip links to the report behind it, and **Ask AI about this** opens the assistant with a question filled in for you to send. Tips are worked out by Fintranzact from your books. They **do not use up your questions**, and they show even when your monthly questions have run out. They only use what **you** are allowed to see: if your role cannot open a report, you get no tip from it. Payroll is never part of tips.

You can **Dismiss for today** on a tip and **Hide** the card; both are remembered in this browser. To switch tips off for yourself, choose **Turn tips off** on the card, or untick **Show tips on my dashboard** in Preferences. This affects only you. If the owner has switched the assistant off for the organisation or your role, you get no tips either.

## Ask "how do I...?"

Ask how to do something in Fintranzact, for example "How do I record a payment?" or "Batch expiry kaise set karein?". The assistant looks in the help centre, gives you a short summary of the steps and links to the article, which opens in a new tab. Questions about your own figures are answered from your books instead. If it cannot find a matching article it says so rather than guessing.

## Have it prepare work for you

Besides answering, the assistant can **prepare** work for you. It never saves anything by itself: every action is shown to you on a **confirmation card**, and nothing is saved until **you** tap **Confirm**.

| You can ask it to | For example |
|---|---|
| Create an invoice | "Create an invoice for Asha Traders: 10 Cotton Fabric", "Asha Traders ke liye invoice banao" |
| Create a quotation | "Create a quotation for Kiran Exports: 5 Silk Saree at 1200" |
| Record a payment | "Record a payment of 5000 from Asha Traders by UPI", "Asha se 5000 ka payment record karo" |
| Add a party | "Add a new customer Meera Stores in Pune, phone 98765 01234" |
| Add an item | "Add a new item Brass Lamp, price 1200, GST 12%" |
| Send a payment reminder | "Send a WhatsApp payment reminder to Asha Traders for invoice INV-12" |

The panel's **Or have me prepare something** examples fill the question box for you to complete; they are not sent until you press Send. You only see examples for what your role can do.

### The confirmation card

The card shows everything that would be saved: the party, the date, each line with its quantity, rate, discount and GST, the totals, the place of supply and any warnings. The figures come from the same calculation Fintranzact uses when it saves, so what you review is what is saved.

- **Confirm** saves it, exactly as if you had used the normal screen, as you. You get a link to the new record.
- **Edit** lets you change dates, quantities, rates, discounts, notes, a payment's amount, mode and reference, or a party's or item's details. The totals are worked out again. You cannot change which party or item it is: ask the assistant, or cancel and start again.
- **Cancel** drops it. Nothing was saved.
- A card **expires after 30 minutes**. After that, ask again.
- If saving is refused (for example the books are locked for that date, or you no longer have permission), the card says why and nothing is saved. Tap Confirm only once: a second tap does not make a second copy.

The assistant picks parties and items by looking them up, never by guessing from a name. If a name matches more than one, it asks you which one. It uses only the amounts, dates and quantities you give it, and asks when something is missing.

### What "send a reminder" means

It does exactly what **Send reminder** on the invoice does. For **email** and **SMS**, confirming sends the message you see on the card. For **WhatsApp**, Fintranzact does not send anything: confirming records the reminder and gives you a button that opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and you press send there.

### It knows the page you are on

If you have an invoice, quotation, party or item open, or a report, the assistant is told which one, so "send this invoice to the customer" or "what does this customer owe?" works. Before it uses that, Fintranzact checks that you are allowed to see it. Pages other than the ones it knows about are not shared with it.

## What you get back

An answer in text, saying which report and period it used. It may add **cards** under the answer: a small table, a simple bar chart, or a link that opens the invoice or report in the app. Links only ever go to pages inside Fintranzact.

Answers stream in as they are written. Press **Stop** to end one early.

## What it cannot do

- It never saves, sends, changes or deletes anything on its own. It can only prepare the actions listed above, and only you can confirm them. It cannot edit or delete an existing invoice, payment or party, and it cannot do anything else in the app: use the normal screens.
- It never makes up numbers. If it cannot find something, it says so.
- It is not a tax or legal adviser. For filing and tax decisions, check with your CA. A GST payable figure comes from your books and may differ from a filed return.
- It does not answer questions that are not about your business or about using Fintranzact.
- Payroll is not included.

## Your permissions still apply

The assistant reads your data **as you**, in the business you have open, with the permissions your role has. If you cannot open a report yourself, the assistant cannot read it for you: it will tell you that you do not have access to that information. It never shows another business's data.

## Questions and limits

- **AI Assistant** includes 150 questions a month and **AI Plus** includes 500. The count resets on the 1st of each month (Indian time).
- A Full Access Trial includes 50 questions in total.
- One question is one message that gets an answer. If the AI service has a problem and you get no answer, the question is not counted. Preparing an action is part of the question that asked for it, and Confirm, Edit and Cancel do not use up a question.
- Extra packs of 100 questions can be added to an organisation. The panel shows how many questions are left.
- When the questions run out, the panel says so. If extra packs are available to buy, the owner sees a **Buy more questions** button and everyone else is told to ask their owner.

These are the prices and rules the AI add-on is planned to launch with. They apply only once the add-on is released; until then none of the buying options below appear.

## Your chats

Each person has their own chat history. Open **Past chats** (the clock) to see yours, open one to continue it, or delete one. Nobody else in your organisation can see your chats, including the owner. A confirmation card in an old chat shows what became of it. An action prepared for you can only be confirmed by you, never by another person, even an admin or the owner.

## Controls for the owner

Owners can open **Settings, then Team, then AI assistant** to switch the assistant off for the whole organisation, or for specific roles (admin, sales manager, salesperson, accountant). A separate switch, **Allow the assistant to prepare actions**, turns the actions off (for everyone, or for specific roles) while keeping the question-and-answer chat. This is enforced by the server, not just hidden on screen. Owners always keep access to the assistant so they can switch it back on.

Every question and each lookup the assistant makes is recorded in the activity log as **via AI assistant**. So is every action it prepares, and what you do with it (edit, confirm, cancel, or a failure), and the record you confirmed (for example the invoice) shows as made **via AI assistant** with your name. The log keeps a short copy of the question and the name of each lookup, not your business figures.

## Privacy

To answer, we send the AI provider your question and only the figures and names needed to answer it. We do not use your data to train AI models. Names and notes in your books are treated as data, never as instructions. Please do not type passwords, PAN, Aadhaar or bank account numbers into the chat: the assistant never asks for them and does not receive them from your books. Voice input uses your browser's own speech recognition and Fintranzact does not receive your audio (see Speak your question above).
