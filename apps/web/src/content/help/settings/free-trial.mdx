Every new organization starts with a **Full Access Trial**. You do not need a card, and there is no free plan to pick.

## What you get during the trial

- **Business-level access.** All the features and limits of the Business plan, whichever plan you picked at sign-up: unlimited businesses and team members, e-invoicing, multiple warehouses, batches and expiry, bank reconciliation, manufacturing, API access and data export.
- **Every add-on, with a cap while you try it:**
  - **AI Assistant:** up to 50 questions.
  - **Payroll:** up to 10 employees.
  - **Store Pro:** your store on your own domain, including connecting the domain.
- **14 days.** If you signed up through a partner's referral link, your trial is **30 days** and the banner says **Your 30-day partner trial**.

The AI Assistant, Payroll and Store Pro features are still being built. Their trial caps are already set, and they apply the moment each feature opens.

## The countdown banner

While the trial runs, a banner at the top of every page counts down, for example **9 days left in your Full Access Trial** with a **Choose a plan** button.

- It starts calm. With **3 days or fewer** left it turns amber.
- Until then you can dismiss it for the day with the **×** button. In the last 3 days it stays, so the end never surprises you.
- Only the organization owner and admins see the **Choose a plan** button. Everyone else is asked to contact the owner.
- It disappears when you subscribe to a plan.

## Reminders

You get an email, and a notice in the bell at the top of the page, when **7 days** are left, when **2 days** are left, and when the trial has ended. For a 14-day trial that is about day 7, day 12 and day 14. Each reminder is sent once, to the organization owner.

## When the trial ends

If you have not chosen a plan, the organization becomes **read-only**, and the banner turns red and says **Trial ended: read-only**.

- You can still **view and search** everything, **download PDFs** and **export your data**.
- You cannot create or edit anything until you buy a plan.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Your data stays exactly as it was.
- **Buying any plan unlocks everything straight away.** Open **Settings → Billing**, pick a plan and pay.

There is no free plan to fall back to.

## One trial per business

A free trial is given once per business. It is checked by email address and by the business GSTIN. If a trial was already used, your new organization is created but starts read-only, with the message **A free trial was already used for this email, phone or GSTIN. Choose a plan to continue.** We never tell you which account used it. Choosing a plan unlocks it right away.

:::note
If you think this is a mistake, contact support. A platform admin can grant a custom trial.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the trial be extended?**
A platform admin can extend a trial or grant a custom one. Contact support and say why you need more time.

**Do I lose my data if I do not subscribe?**
No. Your data is kept. The account is read-only until you choose a plan.

**What happens to my plan choice from sign-up?**
It is the plan you start on when you subscribe. You can pick a different one at checkout.
