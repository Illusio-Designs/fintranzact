If you are an accountant and also a **Fintranzact partner** (you applied under "Accountant" and were approved), your CA work and your partner account connect in three ways. They are linked by your **email address**: use the same one for your login and your partner application.

## The Registered CA partner badge

When a business invites you as its CA, its owner and admins see a small **Registered CA partner** badge next to your name, on the pending invitation and later on the Team list. It tells them you are a vetted Fintranzact partner. You see nothing extra on your own invitation.

The badge appears only for approved accountant partners whose email is verified. Other partner types (reseller, technology) do not get it.

## Clients you manage

Open the **Partner portal**. As an accountant partner you get a **Clients you manage** table with every business where you hold a CA role (**Accountant (read-only)** or **Accountant (filing)**):

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Business | The organization's name |
| Access | Read-only or filing |
| Since | When you accepted the invitation |
| Last opened | When you last opened that client |
| Plan | The client's Fintranzact plan |

It shows only who, what access and when. It never shows a client's invoices, balances or any other books data. If it is empty, no business has invited you yet: ask a client to use **Invite my CA** in their Team settings, then accept with this login. See [Work with many clients from one login](/help/team/many-clients).

## Being credited as a client's partner

Accepting a CA invitation **does not** make you the business's referring partner and **does not** earn commission by itself.

If a client signed up because you recommended Fintranzact, they can say so when they invite you. The **Invite your CA** form has an unticked box: *This CA referred me to Fintranzact. Credit them as my partner.* If they tick it, and when you accept:

- your email still matches your approved accountant partner record,
- and the business has no partner yet,

the business is credited to you like any referral, and your partner stats and commission follow the usual rules for referred businesses. A business that already has a partner is never changed. The credit is recorded in the business's access log. The client cannot tick the box for an email that is not an approved accountant partner.
