Open **Payroll → Loans and advances**. A loan is an amount an employee repays in instalments through payroll. An advance works the same way.

## Issue a loan

Choose **+ Issue loan or advance**: the employee, the amount, the yearly interest (0 for none), **either the number of instalments or the EMI**, the month of the first instalment, the issue date and the purpose. The schedule is shown before you save.

- Interest is on the **reducing balance**, each month: balance x yearly rate / 12, rounded to the paisa.
- The EMI is the standard formula rounded to the paisa; with 0 percent it is the amount divided by the instalments.
- The **last instalment clears whatever is left**, so the loan always ends at exactly zero (the last instalment can differ from the others by a few paise).

A loan starts as **Pending approval**. An **owner or admin**, who did not request it (unless your business has a single user), approves it, then an owner, admin or accountant **disburses** it from a bank or cash account. That posts Dr Loans and Advances to Employees / Cr the bank or cash account, and the account balance goes down.

## Recovery in payroll

Each payroll run deducts the instalments due in its month. They appear on the payslip as **Loan recovery** and **Loan interest** lines.

- At most the **share of net pay** you set on this page (50 percent when you start) is recovered in a month. Check any legal limit on deductions from wages with your CA.
- Whatever cannot be recovered stays due and is **carried forward as arrears** to the next run. The run shows a warning.
- Approving a run records the recovery on each loan. If a loan was changed after the run was calculated, approval asks you to calculate again.
- In the books, principal reduces Loans and Advances to Employees and interest is credited to Interest on Staff Loans, instead of Payroll Deductions Payable.
- An employee with a [full and final settlement](/help/payroll/full-and-final) is not deducted in runs; the balance is recovered there.

## Part-payment, foreclosure, skip, reschedule

- **Record part-payment**: the employee repays part of the principal (and optionally interest). The rest of the loan is re-planned at the same EMI, so it ends sooner.
- **Foreclose**: the employee repays the whole balance; the loan closes.
- **Skip an instalment** (with a reason): the next instalment is not recovered and the rest move one month later. No interest is charged for the skipped month.
- **Reschedule** (with a reason): a new number of instalments or a new EMI, from a month you choose.

Every change is logged, and the **statement** (also as a CSV) lists each event with the balance after it. The employee app does not show loans in this release.

Confirm with your CA how interest on loans to employees is treated for tax.
