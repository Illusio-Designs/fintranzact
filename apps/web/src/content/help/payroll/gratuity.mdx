Open **Payroll → Gratuity** for an estimate of what each employee would be paid **if they left on a date you choose** (today by default), and the total liability.

**Have your CA confirm the formula, the minimum years, the limit and how a provision should be booked.** Fintranzact does not work out income tax on gratuity.

## The formula

Gratuity = last drawn **Basic + DA** x **15 / 26** x years of service.

- **Last drawn wages** are the monthly Basic + DA of the salary structure in force on the date.
- **Years**: complete years of service, plus one more year when the part year is **more than six months**. Exactly six months does not round up.
- **Minimum service**: the number of **completed** years must reach the minimum before anything is payable. Ordinary staff need **5 years**. An employee whose employment type is **Contract** is treated as fixed-term and needs **1 year** (the Labour Codes line in the roadmap). There is **no minimum on death or disablement**: choose those exit reasons when recording the exit. Check with your CA that this treatment fits your contracts.
- **Limit**: the amount is capped at the limit in [Statutory settings](/help/payroll/statutory-settings) (shipped as 20 lakh).

The table shows the completed years, the years used, the rule that applied, the amount payable today, and what the amount would be once the employee becomes eligible.

## Provision (optional)

The page shows the **liability today** (the total payable to eligible employees), the **provision already in your books** and **how much more to provide**. Nothing is booked unless you press **Post provision**:

- Dr Salary - Gratuity / Cr Gratuity Provision for the increase (the other way round if the liability fell).
- Pressing it again with nothing to add posts nothing.
- Only an owner, admin or accountant can post it.

When gratuity is paid in a settlement, it is taken out of the Gratuity Provision first and only the rest is booked as expense.

## Paying gratuity

Gratuity is paid in the [full and final settlement](/help/payroll/full-and-final). The earlier Gratuity register on the Filings tab is a computed list and pays nothing.
