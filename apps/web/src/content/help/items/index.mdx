An **item** in Fintranzact is a product or service that you sell or purchase. Items appear in your invoice line items, and stock quantities are automatically adjusted when you create invoices.

## Item types

| Type | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Physical goods with trackable stock (fabric, electronics, stationery, etc.) |
| **Service** | Work or labour with no physical stock (installation, consulting, delivery charges) |

Service items have stock tracking disabled — their stock quantity is always shown as N/A.

## Item modes

Fintranzact supports three item modes. Choose the right mode when creating the item:

| Mode | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Single unit, single price. Most items fit here. |
| **Alternate Units** | Same item sold or purchased in different units (e.g. cloth sold by metre but tracked in rolls) |
| **Variants** | Same item with multiple configurations (e.g. t-shirt in S/M/L x Red/Blue) |

See [Variants](/help/items/variants) and [Units & Conversions](/help/items/units-and-conversions) for the advanced modes.

:::note[Mobile limitation]
The mobile app only supports creating **Simple** mode items. To create items with variants or alternate units, use the web app. Once created, variant and alternate-unit items can be viewed and used on invoices from the mobile app.
:::

## Adding an item

<ForDesktop>
Go to **Items** in the sidebar and click **+ Add Item** (or press <kbd>N</kbd>). A slide-over panel opens with collapsible Disclosure sections for each group of fields: Item Type, Basic Info, Pricing, Unit, and Stock.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **Items** in the bottom tab bar (if visible) or navigate to the Items section, then tap the floating **+** button. A full-screen form opens with stacked cards for each section:

- **Item Type** — Product or Service toggle
- **Basic Info** — Name, SKU, category, description
- **Pricing** — Sale price, purchase price, and tax preset chips (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) for quick GST rate selection
- **Unit** — Tap to open a unit picker modal with the full list of supported units
- **Stock** — Opening stock quantity and low-stock alert threshold
</ForMobile>

### Required fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Must be unique within your business (case-insensitive) |
| **Item Type** | Product or Service |
| **Unit** | Base unit of measurement. See [Supported Units](/help/reference/supported-units). |

### Important fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Sale Price** | Your default selling price per unit (excluding tax if tax-exclusive) |
| **Purchase Price** | Your default buying price per unit |
| **Tax %** | GST rate: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%. Applied to every invoice line item using this item. |
| **Tax Inclusive** | If enabled, the sale price already includes GST. The taxable value is back-calculated. |
| **HSN Code** | Harmonised System of Nomenclature code (for products) or SAC code (for services). Printed on invoices and used in GSTR-1 HSN summary. |

### Stock tracking fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Stock Quantity** | Current stock (opening stock when you first create the item). Updated automatically on invoices. |
| **Low Stock Alert** | When stock falls at or below this number, the item is flagged in the low-stock view. |

### Other fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **SKU** | Your internal stock-keeping unit number or barcode. Not required but useful for search. |
| **Category** | Free-text category for grouping items (e.g. "Electronics", "Clothing", "Services"). |
| **Description** | Internal notes about the item. Not shown on invoices. |

## Filtering and searching items

<ForDesktop>
The items list provides a search bar at the top and a **SegmentedControl** to filter by type: **All**, **Products**, or **Services**. Items with stock at or below the low-stock threshold display a low-stock badge next to the stock quantity. You can click the **Export** button to download the item list as a CSV file.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The items list provides a search bar at the top and horizontal chip filters for **All**, **Products**, **Services**, and **Low Stock**. Tap the **Low Stock** chip to see only items that need reordering. CSV export is not available on mobile.
</ForMobile>

## How stock is tracked

Stock supports fractional quantities up to 3 decimals (for example, 2.5 kg) and is tracked per [warehouse](/help/inventory/warehouses). The system updates stock in the same transaction as each document:

- **Sale invoice, delivery challan or purchase return saved** → stock decreases
- **Purchase invoice, goods receipt note (GRN) or sales return saved** → stock increases
- **Credit notes, debit notes, quotations, proforma invoices and orders** → no change
- **Document edited** → only the difference is applied (change 10 to 12 and 2 more go out)
- **Document cancelled or deleted** → the stock change is reversed; reinstating a cancelled document applies it again

Drafts move stock too. An invoice made from a delivery challan or GRN doesn't move stock a second time. See [How Stock Moves](/help/inventory/how-stock-moves) for the full rules, the warehouse picker and the negative stock setting.

## Stock adjustments

You can manually adjust stock outside of invoices. Common uses:
- Physical inventory count correction
- Writing off damaged or expired goods
- Recording stock found or given away as samples

<ForDesktop>
For adjustments at a particular warehouse, or several items at once, use **Inventory → Stock Adjustments** (see [Stock Adjustments](/help/inventory/stock-adjustments)). To count a whole warehouse by scanning, use [Physical Stock](/help/inventory/physical-stock).

For a quick fix to one item: open the item detail in the slide-over panel, then click **Adjust Stock**. A panel opens where you enter the adjustment details. This always adjusts your **Opening stock & adjustments** default warehouse.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
To adjust stock: open the item detail screen, then tap **Adjust Stock**. A modal appears where you enter the adjustment details.
</ForMobile>

Enter the following fields:
- **Add Stock** or **Remove Stock**, and the **Quantity**
- **Adjustment date**: defaults to today, but you can backdate the adjustment
- **Reason** (optional): for example, "Physical count correction" or "Goods damaged"

Every adjustment is logged with the previous stock, new stock, quantity change, reason, date, and the user who made the change. You can view the full adjustment history on the item detail page. For items with variants, you can adjust stock per variant individually.

## Low-stock alerts

Items where `stockQuantity <= lowStockAlert` appear in the **Low Stock** filter on the items list. Use this to quickly see what needs reordering.

:::tip
Set a low-stock alert threshold on every item you actively purchase. Check the low-stock view weekly before placing purchase orders.
:::

## Online store visibility

Each item can be enabled for your [Online Store](/help/online-store). When enabled, you can set:
- A separate **Store Price** (defaults to sale price if not set)
- A **Store Category** (different from the internal category)
- A **Store Description** (customer-facing, different from internal description)
- A **Sort Order** (controls the order items appear in the store)

Stock management for the store follows the same rules as regular invoices. If the store is running low on stock, it appears in the low-stock view.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have the same item name for different businesses?**
Yes. Item names are unique per business, not across your entire Fintranzact account.

**Can I change an item's mode after creation?**
No. The item mode (simple, alternate units, variants) is fixed at creation time. If you need a different mode, create a new item.

**Does Fintranzact support negative stock?**
You choose. On **Inventory → Warehouses**, the **Selling more than you have** setting can be **Allow** (stock can go below zero silently), **Warn** (the default: the form shows a shortfall warning but saving still works) or **Block** (documents that would take a warehouse below zero can't be saved). See [How Stock Moves](/help/inventory/how-stock-moves).

**How do I set the HSN code for a service item?**
Services use **SAC** (Services Accounting Code) rather than HSN. Enter the SAC code in the HSN field — both are 4-8 digit codes and are handled identically by Fintranzact.
