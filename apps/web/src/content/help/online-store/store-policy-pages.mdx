Payment providers such as Razorpay check that an online store has its policy pages before they approve online payments. Fintranzact gives every store five pages, each with its own public link, and fills them in from your business details so you are not starting from a blank page.

The five pages are:

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Terms & Conditions** | Orders, prices, payments, changes to the terms, disputes |
| **Refund & Cancellation** | Cancelling an order, the return window, how refunds are paid |
| **Shipping & Delivery** | Where you deliver, delivery time, charges, damaged parcels |
| **Contact Us** | Your business name, address, GSTIN, phone and email |
| **Privacy Policy** | What customer information you collect, why, who it is shared with, and customers' choices under Indian data protection law |

## Where the pages show up

- In the **footer** of every page of your store.
- On the **checkout** page, next to the Place Order button.
- At their own links under your store address, for example `https://store.fintranzact.com/myshop/policies/refund`.

The links are the store address followed by `/policies/` and one of `terms`, `refund`, `shipping`, `contact` or `privacy`. Each page can also be opened as a plain, fast-loading page without scripts (the **plain page** link next to each address). That is the version a reviewer's tool or a search engine can always read.

## Edit your policy pages

1. Go to **Settings → Online Store** and scroll to **Policy pages**.
2. Choose a page from the tabs.
3. Read the text, change what does not fit how you work, and click **Save page**.
4. Use **Preview** to see the page the way customers will.

A page you have not edited is marked **Using the template** and keeps following your business details: if you change your phone number or address, the page changes with it. Once you save your own text, the page is marked **Edited** with the date.

**Reset to template** throws away your text for that page and goes back to the default.

### Placeholders

The templates use placeholders that fill in automatically. You can use them in your own text too.

| Placeholder | Filled with |
|---|---|
| `{{businessName}}` | Your business name |
| `{{address}}` | Your address, city, state and pincode |
| `{{gstin}}` | Your GSTIN (only if your business is GST registered) |
| `{{phone}}` | Your business phone number |
| `{{email}}` | Your business email |
| `{{returnWindowDays}}` | Your return window, in days |
| `{{deliveryCharges}}` | Your delivery charge from Settings, for example "Rs 49 per order, plus GST where applicable, free on orders of Rs 500 or more". Left out when you do not charge for delivery. See [Delivery charges](/help/online-store/delivery-charges). |

If a detail is missing, for example you have no phone number saved, the line that uses it is left out instead of showing an empty label. Click **Fill in my details** if you would rather edit the page as plain text with your details already typed in. After that the page no longer follows your business profile.

### Formatting

Policy text is plain text with a few simple marks:

- `## Heading` makes a heading.
- `- item` makes a bulleted list, and `1. item` a numbered one.
- `**bold**` makes bold text.
- `[text](https://example.com)` makes a link. Only `http`, `https`, `mailto` and `tel` links work.

Anything else, including HTML tags, is shown as plain text. It is never run on your store.

## Set your return window

The return window is the number of days a customer has to ask for a return. Set it in **Settings → Online Store → Return Window (days)**. The default is 7 days. It appears in the Refund & Cancellation and Shipping & Delivery pages.

## Give the links to Razorpay

The **Public links for Razorpay** box at the top of the section lists all five addresses. Use **Copy all links** to copy them in one go, or the **Copy** button next to a single page. Your store must be switched on and have a store URL for the links to work; for anyone else they show "Store not found".

## What goes on the public pages

The pages show what you put in them. The templates use your business name, address, phone, email and, if you are GST registered, your GSTIN. If you do not want one of these shown, remove that line from the page.

## A note on the templates

The templates are plain-language starting points for a small online shop in India. They are not legal advice. Read each page, correct anything that does not match your business (for example a no-returns item, or delivery only in your city), and ask your own adviser if you need wording checked.

## Plan

Policy pages are part of the online store, so they follow the same plan as the store itself.
