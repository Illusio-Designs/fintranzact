Plans in Fintranzact belong to your **organization** (the account that holds all your businesses and team members), not to an individual business. Every business and team member in the organization shares the organization's plan.

## Available plans

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Forever Free** | ₹0 | Everyone. Unlimited for life — the recommended plan for new sign-ups. |
| **Pro** | Custom (priced on request) | Growing teams that want advanced automation, priority support, and expanded collaboration. |
| **Business** | Custom (priced on request) | Larger organizations that want multi-tenant controls, premium reporting, and dedicated onboarding. |

The full comparison, including the exact limits each plan enforces, is on the public [pricing page](/pricing). The numbers on that page are read from the same plan definitions the server uses to enforce limits.

### What Forever Free includes

The Forever Free plan has no numeric limits:

- Unlimited invoices, parties, and payments
- Unlimited businesses and team members
- Unlimited organizations you can own
- Unlimited devices signed in at the same time
- Unlimited API keys (for the [CLI and MCP server](/help/ai))
- Unlimited recurring invoice runs
- Full data export and the online store
- No "Powered by Fintranzact" branding on invoice PDFs

Every feature of the product — GST invoicing, e-invoicing and e-way bills, GST returns, inventory, banking and reconciliation, reports — is available on every plan.

## Choosing a plan after sign-up

When a new organization is created, Fintranzact shows the plan picker (**Choose your plan — Select the plan that fits your business**) before you can use the app. You also land on this screen right after you create a business.

1. Click a plan card to see what it includes in the **Selected** panel on the right.
2. If you picked **Forever Free**, click **Create your company**.
3. If you picked **Pro** or **Business**, the panel explains that the plan is set up by the Fintranzact team. Click **Start free and create your company** — your organization starts on Forever Free and the team switches it to the paid plan once it's arranged.

:::note[Paid plans are not self-serve]
There is no in-app checkout. Owners can choose the free plan themselves; Pro and Business are switched on by the Fintranzact team after you [contact them](/contact) for a quote. Your data stays exactly where it is when the plan changes.
:::

:::caution
If your organization is already on a paid plan and you see the plan picker again (for example, after creating another business), don't pick a different plan just to get past the screen — contact support if you're unsure which plan your organization is on.
:::

## The older Free plan

Organizations created before Forever Free was introduced may still be on the older **Free** plan. It is not offered to new sign-ups and has these limits:

| Limit | Free (older plan) |
|---|---|
| Organizations you can own | 1 |
| Businesses per organization | 1 |
| Team members (including pending invitations) | 3 |
| Devices signed in at once | 3 — signing in on a 4th device signs out the oldest session |
| API keys | None — the CLI and MCP server are not available |
| Recurring invoice runs | 5 per month, per business |
| Audit log history | 30 days |
| Full data export | Not available |
| Online store | Not available |
| Invoice PDFs | Show "Powered by Fintranzact" |

When you hit one of these limits, the app shows a message such as "Your plan allows up to 1 business. Upgrade to add more." Contact the Fintranzact team to move to Forever Free or a paid plan.

## When your trial ends or a payment fails

If your trial ends, your plan ends, or a renewal payment fails and the grace period runs out, your organization becomes **read-only**. A banner at the top of every page says so. Nothing is deleted, and you can still view, search, download PDFs and export your data. Creating and editing is paused, and the app shows **Choose a plan** when you try.

- **Owners:** click **Choose a plan** (or open **Settings → Billing**) and subscribe. Access returns as soon as the payment goes through.
- **Everyone else:** the banner asks you to contact your organization owner, who is the only one who can pick a plan.
- **A renewal payment failed:** you get a few days of grace first, with a warning banner showing how long you have and an **Update payment** button.
- **Trial:** in the last 7 days a banner shows how many days are left. You can dismiss it for the day.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a credit card to sign up?**
No. You sign up with your email or phone number and start on Forever Free. No payment details are collected in the app.

**Is the free plan really unlimited?**
Yes. Forever Free has no time limit, no invoice cap, no cap on businesses or team members, and no Fintranzact branding on your documents.

**How do I upgrade to Pro or Business?**
Get in touch with the Fintranzact team through the contact page. Paid plans are priced to your team size and needs; once arranged, the team switches your organization's plan. You don't need to re-enter any data.

**Does each of my businesses need its own plan?**
No. The plan is set on the organization, so every business inside it is covered.

<ForCA>
**I manage many client businesses. Which plan do I need?**
Forever Free already allows unlimited businesses and team members in one organization, so you can add all your clients' businesses and invite your staff without upgrading. Consider Pro or Business if you need priority support or dedicated onboarding for your firm.
</ForCA>

<ForBusiness>
**Will my customers see Fintranzact branding on my invoices?**
Not on Forever Free or the paid plans. Only organizations still on the older Free plan have "Powered by Fintranzact" on their invoice PDFs.
</ForBusiness>
