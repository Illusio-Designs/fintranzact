The Banking section tracks the balances of your business bank accounts, UPI accounts, and cash. When you link a payment to a bank account, Fintranzact records the deposit or withdrawal for you.

## Account types

| Type | Use for |
|---|---|
| `savings` | Standard savings bank account |
| `current` | Current account (business banking) |
| `cash` | Physical cash, such as a petty cash box or counter cash |
| `upi` | UPI-linked account (Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm business accounts) |
| `credit_card` | Business credit card |
| `payment_gateway` | Razorpay, PayU, or another gateway that deducts charges before paying out. See [Payment Gateway Accounts](/help/banking/payment-gateways). |

## Adding a bank account

<ForDesktop>
Go to **Cash & Bank** in the sidebar and click **+ Add Account**. The **Add Bank Account** panel opens. Fill in the account details and click **Create Account**.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **More** in the bottom tab bar, then tap **Bank**. Tap the floating **+** button. The **New Bank Account** screen opens. Fill in the account details and tap **Create Account**.
</ForMobile>

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Account Type** | Yes | Savings, Current, Cash, UPI, Credit Card, or Payment Gateway |
| **Account Name** | Yes | A label for the account, for example "SBI Current Account" or "GPay UPI" |
| **Account Number** | No | For savings, current, and credit card accounts. For a credit card, the web app labels it **Last 4 Digits**. |
| **IFSC Code** | No | For savings and current accounts. Bank reconciliation also uses it to recognise your bank's statement format. |
| **UPI ID** | No | Web app, UPI accounts only. For example `business@upi`. It is used for the UPI QR code on invoices. |
| **Bank Name** | No | Name of the bank |
| **Opening Balance (₹)** | No | Your account balance on the day you start using Fintranzact. Defaults to 0. |
| **Set as default account** | No | If on, this account is auto-selected when you record payments |

<ForMobile>
The mobile form shows **Bank Name**, **Account Number**, and **IFSC Code** for every account type except Payment Gateway. It has no separate UPI ID field.
</ForMobile>

## Default account

You can set one account as your default. It is auto-selected when you record a payment. When an expense is posted to a bank account automatically, the default account is preferred among accounts of the right type.

<ForDesktop>
To change the default, hover over an account in the **Accounts** list and click the edit icon. Turn on **Set as default account**, then click **Save Changes**.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Open an account and tap **Set as Default**. You can also turn on the switch on the account's edit screen.
</ForMobile>

## Banking layout

<ForDesktop>
The **Cash & Bank** page has three summary cards at the top: **Total Balance**, **Cash in Hand**, and **Bank Balance**. Bank Balance covers every account that is not a cash account. Below the cards, the **Accounts** list is on the left and the selected account's **Transactions** are on the right. Click an account to see its history. Use the period buttons (**This Month**, **Last Month**, **Last 30 Days**, **This FY**, **Last FY**, **Custom**, **All**) to filter by date, and **Export CSV** to download the list.

Payments that are not linked to any account appear under **Untracked Payments** at the bottom of the page. Filter them by mode, select them, and assign them to an account in bulk.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The **Cash & Bank** screen opens with a summary card showing **Total Balance**, plus **Cash in Hand** and **Bank Balance**. Below it is a list of account cards showing each account's name, type, and current balance. Tap an account card to open its details and transaction history. Tap **Load more** to see older transactions.
</ForMobile>

## How balances are tracked

Fintranzact updates each account's current balance automatically:

- **Payment received** (a customer pays you): balance goes up by the payment amount
- **Payment made** (you pay a supplier against a purchase invoice): balance goes down by the payment amount
- **Expense**: balance goes down by the expense amount
- **Bank transfer in**: balance goes up by the transfer amount
- **Bank transfer out**: balance goes down by the transfer amount
- **Manual transaction**: balance goes up for a deposit and down for a withdrawal

Each change creates a bank transaction, which gives you a full ledger of what moved through each account.

:::note
When you record an expense, Fintranzact picks the account from the payment mode:

- **Cash** uses a cash account.
- **Bank** and **Cheque** use a savings or current account.
- **UPI** uses a UPI account.

The default account is preferred when there is more than one. Expenses with the mode **Other** are not posted to any account.
:::

## Bank transfers

<ForDesktop>
Click the **Transfer** button to open the **Transfer Money** panel. Select the **From Account** and **To Account**, enter the amount, and confirm. This creates:
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap the **Transfer** icon in the header to open the **Transfer Funds** screen. Tap each account field to open a picker and choose the source and destination accounts. Enter the amount and confirm. This creates:

Both transactions reference each other so you can trace them. The transfer is saved as a single step, so the two balances always stay consistent.

## Reconciling your balance

To check your books against the bank, use [Bank Reconciliation](/help/banking/bank-reconciliation) in the web or desktop app. It is under **Accounts → Bank Reconciliation** in the sidebar.

1. Import your bank statement as a CSV. Built-in templates cover SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank, IDBI, and IndusInd.
2. Fintranzact auto-matches each statement line with your payments, expenses, and bank transactions.
3. Review the matches. Match any leftover lines by hand, turn bank charges into expenses, or ignore lines.
4. The **Summary (BRS)** tab compares the book balance with the statement balance.

For bank charges or interest that appear only on the statement, add a manual transaction with **+ Add Transaction** so the account balance stays accurate.

## UPI details on invoices

If you have a **UPI** account with a **UPI ID**, Fintranzact adds a UPI payment QR code and link to your sale invoices. The QR code asks for the invoice's unpaid balance. Customers can scan it with any UPI app to pay. The QR code appears only on sale invoices that still have a balance due.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I track multiple bank accounts?**
Yes. Add as many accounts as you need. Each has its own transaction history and balance.

**I entered the wrong opening balance. Can I correct it?**
Yes. Edit the account and change **Opening Balance (₹)**. The running **Balance** shown next to each transaction is recalculated from the new opening balance. The account's current balance is not recalculated, though. To correct it, add a manual deposit or withdrawal for the difference.

**Can I record inter-bank transfers (moving money from one account to another)?**
Yes. Use the **Transfer** feature in the Banking section. Select the source account, the destination account, and the amount. Fintranzact creates a withdrawal in the source account and a deposit in the destination account in a single step, keeping both balances accurate.

**Does Fintranzact connect to my bank to import transactions automatically?**
No. Fintranzact has no live bank feed. You can download your statement as a CSV from net banking and import it on the [Bank Reconciliation](/help/banking/bank-reconciliation) page in the web or desktop app. Fintranzact then matches the statement lines with your payments and expenses. Everything else is recorded through payments, expenses, transfers, and manual transactions.

## Payment Gateway Accounts

Fintranzact can track charges from Razorpay, PayU, PhonePe, and other payment gateways automatically. When a customer pays through a gateway, Fintranzact records the gateway charge as an expense and can move the net amount to your bank account.

See [Payment Gateway Accounts](/help/banking/payment-gateways) for setup and configuration.
