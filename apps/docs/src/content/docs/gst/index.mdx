Fintranzact is designed to make GST compliance as automatic as possible. This page gives you an overview of how GST flows through the system from invoice creation to report generation.

## How GST is calculated on invoices

See [GST on Invoices](/invoicing/gst-on-invoices) for the full explanation. In summary:

1. You set a tax rate (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%) on each item.
2. You set your business's 2-digit state code in Settings.
3. You set each party's 2-digit state code on their party record.
4. When you create an invoice, Fintranzact compares state codes to determine CGST+SGST (same state) or IGST (different states).

## GST report generation

Fintranzact generates two key GST reports: **GSTR-1** and **GSTR-3B**. Both are computed from your invoice and payment data — there is no manual data entry into the reports.

Reports cover outward supplies (sales) for GSTR-1 and both outward (sales) and inward (purchases) supplies for the ITC calculation in GSTR-3B.

:::note
GST reports are available for **all** businesses — both GST-registered and unregistered. For unregistered businesses, the reports show total sales and purchase figures without GST breakdown. For registered businesses, full CGST/SGST/IGST splits are shown.
:::

## GSTR-1

**GSTR-1** is the monthly return for outward supplies (sales). Fintranzact generates it with the following sections:

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **B2B** | Sale invoices to GST-registered parties (parties with a GSTIN) |
| **B2C Large** | Inter-state sale invoices to unregistered parties exceeding ₹2.5 lakh |
| **B2C Small** | Intra-state and small inter-state sales to unregistered parties, aggregated by tax rate |
| **HSN Summary** | Total quantity and value sold, broken down by HSN code |
| **Credit Notes** | Credit note documents issued during the period |
| **Debit Notes** | Debit note documents issued during the period |

See [GSTR-1](/gst/gstr1) for detailed instructions.

## GSTR-3B

**GSTR-3B** is the monthly summary return. Fintranzact generates:

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **3.1 Outward Supplies** | Total taxable value and tax broken down by IGST, CGST, SGST |
| **3.2 Inter-state to unregistered** | State-wise IGST on large inter-state B2C supplies |
| **4 ITC (Input Tax Credit)** | GST paid on purchase invoices |
| **Tax Payable** | Total output tax (from sales) |
| **Net Tax** | Tax payable minus ITC = tax you owe to the government |

## CSV export

You can download GSTR-1 as a CSV file for your own review or to hand to your CA. To export it, open the **GSTR-1** tab for the month you want and click **Export GSTR-1 CSV**. The file is named like `GSTR1_Apr_2025.csv`.

The CSV has a header (period, GSTIN, business name) followed by three blocks, separated by blank lines:

- **B2B**: one row per invoice
- **B2CS**: one row per tax rate
- **Summary**: invoice count and taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST, tax and invoice totals

B2CL, the HSN summary, and credit and debit notes aren't included in the CSV. The CSV isn't in the GST portal's upload format either.

## Current limitations

- **GSTR-1 from the web app is a generic CSV only.** The portal needs a JSON file (or the offline utility), so the CSV has to be re-keyed or converted. A portal-format GSTR-1 JSON is generated by the API (`gst.gstr1Json`), but the web app has no button for it. For the annual return, the **GSTR-9** tab does have a **Download Portal JSON (GSTN)** button.
- **Zero-rated and exempt supplies aren't tracked.** SEZ supplies and exports appear as regular B2B or inter-state supplies (every B2B row has type "Regular"). The *Zero-rated supplies* and *Exempt supplies* rows on the GSTR-3B tab always show ₹0.
- **No GSTR-4 for composition dealers.** A quarterly CMP-08 calculation exists only through the API (`gst.cmp08`). It always applies a flat 1% rate to taxable sales, whatever the business category. Prepare GSTR-4 and CMP-08 outside Fintranzact.
- **Items without an HSN code are reported under HSN `0000`** in the GSTR-1 HSN summary. Set an HSN or SAC code on every item.

## E-filing tools

Besides the returns on the **GST Returns** page, GST-registered businesses get four compliance tools in the web and desktop app, under **Reports** in the sidebar:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [E-Invoicing](/gst/e-invoicing) | Registers B2B sale invoices with the IRP to get an IRN and signed QR code, and lets you retry failures and cancel IRNs |
| [E-Way Bills](/gst/eway-bills) | Generates e-way bills for goods invoices of ₹50,000 or more, updates the vehicle (Part-B), cancels bills and tracks expiry |
| [GSTR-2B Reconciliation](/gst/gstr2b) | Uploads the portal's GSTR-2B and matches it against your purchase invoices |
| [Input Tax Credit](/gst/itc) | ITC ledger, Section 17(5) blocking, 180-day aging alerts, utilization and the GSTR-3B Table 4 view |

## Filing workflow (recommended)

<ForBusiness>
At the end of each month:

1. Open **GST** in the left navigation and pick the month.
2. Glance at the GSTR-1 totals — do they match roughly what you sold? If a number looks off, the most common cause is a cancelled invoice or a party with the wrong state code.
3. Export the CSV and send it to your CA. That's it on your side — your CA handles the actual portal upload.
4. Check the **GSTR-3B** tab to see your net tax liability before the 20th, so you know how much to keep in the bank.
</ForBusiness>

<ForCA>
:::tip[CA filing workflow]
1. Open the client's business, select **GST** → **GSTR-1**, pick the month.
2. Verify B2B invoice count and aggregate tax against the client's books. Cancelled invoices are excluded; draft invoices are **included** if dated in the period — flag drafts before filing.
3. Export the CSV. Re-format into the GST Offline Utility's template (or use your firm's conversion macro) and generate the JSON.
4. Upload JSON to the GST portal by the **11th**. File GSTR-3B by the **20th** after reviewing ITC in the 3B tab.
5. For amendments, correct the invoice in Fintranzact, re-generate GSTR-1, and report the delta in the next month's amendment tables on the portal.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**My GSTIN is not showing on the invoice PDF. What is wrong?**
Go to Settings → Business Profile and confirm that:
1. Your GST Registration Type is set to "Regular" or "Composition" (not "Unregistered").
2. Your GSTIN is entered correctly in the GSTIN field.

**A sale invoice is not appearing in the GSTR-1 report. Why?**
Cancelled invoices are excluded from all GST reports. Check the invoice status. Also check that the invoice date falls within the reporting period and that the document type is "invoice" (quotations and delivery challans are not included in GSTR-1).

**How do I handle invoices for the previous month that I created this month?**
GSTR-1 uses the invoice date, not the creation date. If you create an invoice today but set the invoice date to last month, it will appear in last month's GSTR-1 report.
