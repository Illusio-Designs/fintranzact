Fintranzact uses email-based invitations to add new members to your organization. This page covers the full invitation lifecycle, from sending to acceptance.

## Invitation lifecycle

1. An Admin or Owner creates an invitation from **Settings → Team** by entering the invitee's email and selecting a role.
2. An invitation email is sent automatically. A copyable link is also displayed in the UI as a fallback.
3. The invitee clicks the link.

<ForDesktop>
The invitation token is stored in the browser and the invitee is redirected to the login page.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
If the Fintranzact app is installed, the invitation deep link opens the app directly. If the app is not installed, the link opens in the device's browser and prompts the invitee to download the app or continue on the web.
</ForMobile>

4. **New user:** signs up with a username, email and password, and the invitation is auto-accepted. They land directly in the invited organization.
5. **Existing user:** clicks the link, the invitation is accepted immediately, and the invited organization is auto-selected in their session.
6. The invitation expires after **7 days** if not accepted.

## Invite your CA

Your accountant (CA) can have their own login to your books without sharing your password. Only the **owner** of the organization can invite a CA; admins cannot.

<ForDesktop>
1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite my CA** (next to **Invite member**).
2. Enter your CA's email address. Capital letters don't matter.
3. Choose what they can do:
   - **View only** (Accountant, read-only): can view everything and download reports. Cannot change anything.
   - **View and file returns** (Accountant, filing): can view everything, prepare and file GST returns, and download reports. Cannot create or edit sales, purchases, payments or other records.
4. Click **Send invite**. We email your CA, and you also get a link you can copy and send yourself.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
1. Open **More → Settings → Team** and tap **Invite my CA**.
2. Enter your CA's email address and choose **View only** or **View and file returns**.
3. Tap **Send Invitation**. We email your CA and the invitation shows as pending until they accept.
</ForMobile>

Good to know:

- The invitation email says you have invited them as your accountant, shows the access level, and tells them you can remove access at any time and that their activity is logged.
- Accountants don't use up your team-member limit, and pending CA invitations don't either. An organization can have up to **3** accountants (including pending invitations).
- Only the owner can change a member to or from an accountant role in the Team list, and remove access by removing the member. Accountant roles appear with a small **CA** badge.
- If your CA is a Fintranzact partner, you see a **Registered CA partner** badge on their invitation and on the Team list. On the web form, an unticked box (*This CA referred me to Fintranzact. Credit them as my partner.*) lets you credit them as your partner when they accept; leave it off and nothing changes. See [CA partners and your clients](/help/team/ca-partners).
- If your CA signs up through your link, they can also create their own firm afterwards to manage all their clients in one place.
- Accountant roles are covered by the "owners, admins and accountants" two-factor policy.

## Accepting invitations

<ForDesktop>
When you click an invitation link in your browser, the token is stored in your session. If you are already signed in, the invitation is accepted immediately and the invited organization appears in your business switcher. If you are not signed in, you are redirected to the login page — the invitation is accepted automatically after you sign in.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
When you tap an invitation link on your phone, the Fintranzact app opens directly via deep linking (if installed). The invitation is accepted automatically after you sign in or complete sign-up. The invited organization is then selected as your active business. If the app is not installed, the link opens in your mobile browser where you can accept the invitation on the web.
</ForMobile>

## Pending invitations

After sending an invitation, it appears in the **Pending Invitations** section at the top of the Team settings page. Each pending invitation shows:

- The invitee's email address
- The assigned role
- When the invitation was sent

Admins and Owners can **revoke** any pending invitation at any time. Revoking invalidates the invitation link immediately.

Pending invitations are also accessible via the CLI and MCP server:

- CLI: `fintranzact tenant invitations`
- MCP: `tenant_pending_invitations` tool

## What happens to new users

When a user signs up through an invitation link, **no auto-created organization** is generated for them. They join directly into the invited organization with the role that was set at invitation time.

Available roles at invitation time:

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `admin` | Full access to all businesses and settings |
| `seller_manager` | Manage the sales team and see all invoices |
| `seller` | Create invoices and record payments |
| `accountant` | Accountant (bookkeeping): payments, expenses, bank; read-only elsewhere |

Accountant roles for your CA (`auditor` and `ca_filing`) are invited with **Invite my CA**, see above.

After accepting, the invited organization is automatically selected in the user's session.

## Email notifications

Invitation emails are sent automatically using:

- **Production:** Resend (transactional email service)
- **Development:** console log output

Each invitation email contains:

- The inviter's name (accountant invitations say "has invited you as their accountant" and show the access level)
- The organization name
- An **Accept** button linking to the app
- A 7-day expiry notice

If email delivery fails, the copyable link displayed in the Team settings UI is always available as a fallback. Share it with the invitee directly.

## Plan limits

The number of team members (including pending invitations) is limited by your plan:

| Plan | Max Team Members |
|------|-----------------|
| Free | 3 (including pending invites) |
| Pro | 15 |
| Business | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Accountant (CA) members and invitations are not counted. If you reach the limit, you must upgrade your plan or revoke an existing invitation before sending a new one.

## CLI and MCP reference

### CLI

```bash
# List pending invitations
fintranzact tenant invitations
fintranzact tenant invitations --json

# Revoke a pending invitation
fintranzact tenant revoke-invitation <invitationId>
```

### MCP tools

- `tenant_pending_invitations` — list all pending (unaccepted, non-expired) invitations
- `tenant_revoke_invitation` — revoke a pending invitation by UUID

See [CLI reference](/help/ai/cli) and [MCP Server reference](/help/ai/mcp-server) for full details.
