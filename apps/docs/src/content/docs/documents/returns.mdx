Use a return when goods physically move back:

| | Sales Return | Purchase Return |
|---|---|---|
| Use it when | A customer sends goods back to you | You send goods back to a supplier |
| Default number | `SR-00001` | `PR-00001` |
| Statuses | Draft, Sent, Cancelled | Draft, Sent, Cancelled |
| Stock | Comes **in**, into your sales-return warehouse | Goes **out**, from your purchase-return warehouse |
| Party ledger | Credit — the customer owes you less | Debit — you owe the supplier less |
| GSTR-1 | **Credit Notes** section | **Debit Notes** section |
| Where to create | Web and mobile app | [CLI](/ai/cli) or [MCP server](/ai/mcp-server) only |

If nothing comes back and you are only correcting the amount, use a [credit note](/documents/credit-and-debit-notes) instead.

## Sales returns

### Creating a return against an invoice

Creating the return from the invoice links the two, so the invoice's balance goes down and GSTR-1 shows the original invoice number.

<ForDesktop>
1. Go to **Invoices** and open the sale invoice.
2. Click **Create Sales Return**. It is shown once the invoice is no longer a draft, is not cancelled, and has not been fully credited or returned.
3. The **New Sales Return** panel opens pre-filled with the invoice's customer, lines, notes and terms, dated today.
4. Keep only the lines and quantities that came back.
5. If you have more than one active warehouse, choose **Receive into**. It starts at the sales-return warehouse set in your inventory settings.
6. Click **Create Sales Return**.

The invoice's detail panel then shows a **See SR-…** link to the return.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
1. Open the invoice from the **Invoices** tab.
2. Tap **Create Sales Return**. It is hidden for drafts, cancelled invoices and invoices that are fully adjusted.
3. The **New Sales Return** screen opens pre-filled with the customer and items, dated today (**Return Date**).
4. Adjust **Items (Returned)** and add a reason in **Notes (optional)**.
5. Check the **Return Amount** and tap **Create Sales Return**.

The screen reminds you: *Creating a sales return will increment stock (goods returned by customer).*
</ForMobile>

:::caution
**Create Sales Return** is also shown on **purchase** invoices on the web. Don't use it there: it creates a sales return on the purchases side that **adds** stock instead of removing it, and it won't appear on the **Sales Returns** page (which shows sales-side returns only). To send goods back to a supplier, use a purchase return.
:::

### How a sales return adjusts the invoice

Sales returns and credit notes share one limit per invoice:

- Together they cannot exceed the invoice total. If they would, saving fails with *Amount exceeds remaining adjustable amount*.
- The invoice's balance due goes down by the return's total.
- Once they cover the whole invoice, the invoice shows as **Adjusted**.

Cancelling or deleting the return takes the stock back out and removes it from the invoice's adjusted amount.

### Standalone sales returns

<ForDesktop>
Go to **VOUCHERS** → **Sales Returns** and click **+ New Sales Return**. The form has no field to link an invoice, so the return reduces the customer's overall balance but not any particular invoice.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **More** → **Sales returns**, then the **+** button. Pick the customer, set the **Return Date**, add items and tap **Create Sales Return**. The return is not linked to an invoice.
</ForMobile>

### Managing sales returns

<ForDesktop>
The **Sales Returns** page has status tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Sent** and **Cancelled**. Columns are **Party**, **Return #**, **Date**, **Ref. Invoice** (**Linked** when made from an invoice), **Status** and **Total**. Draft rows offer **Mark Sent** and **Delete** (Admin or Owner). Click a row to see its details, the **Reference Invoice** link, and a **Share link** section. Drafts can be edited from the detail panel.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The **Sales returns** screen has chips **All**, **Draft**, **Sent**, **Refunded** and **Cancelled**. Cards offer **Mark Sent** and **Delete** as drafts, and **Cancel** once sent. Tap a card to see the customer, **Return Date**, **Source Invoice**, **Items (Returned)**, the **Return Amount**, and, for drafts, **Mark as Sent** and **Delete Sales Return**.

The **Refunded** chip currently shows an error, because sales returns have no refunded status.
</ForMobile>

:::caution
On the web form, the whole-document **Discount** box is not saved on returns, and **Round Off** only counts toward the saved total when the return also has a charge.
:::

## Purchase returns

Purchase returns have no page in the web or mobile app. Create them with the [CLI](/ai/cli) or the [MCP server](/ai/mcp-server). When you pass the original purchase invoice as the reference, the same rules as sales returns apply: the return counts toward that invoice's adjustable amount, and the invoice shows as **Adjusted** once fully covered.

A purchase return:

- takes the goods **out** of stock, from the purchase-return warehouse in your inventory settings (or the warehouse you pass);
- reduces what you owe the supplier in the [party ledger](/parties/party-ledger);
- is posted in the derived accounts as **Dr Payable / Cr Purchase Returns + Input GST**;
- appears in the **Debit Notes** section of GSTR-1.

Warehouses and the default return warehouses are covered in [Inventory](/items/inventory/).

<ForCA>
:::caution[GST treatment to check]
- Sales returns appear under GSTR-1 **Credit Notes** but are **not** deducted from GSTR-1 totals or GSTR-3B outward tax. Adjust 3B yourself.
- Purchase returns appear under GSTR-1 **Debit Notes**, although they are inward documents. Remove them from the CDNR you file.
- GSTR-3B's ITC total currently **adds** the tax on purchase returns instead of reversing it. Reverse that ITC manually.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**Where does the returned stock go?**
Into the warehouse chosen in **Receive into** on the web form. If you don't pick one, the sales-return warehouse from your inventory settings is used. Returns created on mobile always use that default.

**Can I return more than I sold?**
Not against a linked invoice by value: sales returns and credit notes together can't exceed the invoice total. Quantities are not checked line by line.

**I cancelled a sales return. Is the stock removed again?**
Yes. Cancelling or deleting a return reverses its stock movement.

**Why can't I find the Purchase Returns page?**
There isn't one yet. Use the CLI or MCP server, or ask your developer to create them through the API.

**Does a sales return show on the customer's statement?**
Yes. It appears in the party ledger as a credit that reduces what the customer owes.
