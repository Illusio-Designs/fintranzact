A **warehouse** is any place you keep stock: your main godown, a second godown in another city, a shop counter, or a storeroom. Fintranzact tracks how much of each item sits in each warehouse, so you always know not just *how much* you have, but *where* it is.

Open it from the sidebar: **Inventory → Warehouses**.

:::note[Web app only]
Warehouses, and the other inventory screens in this section, are available in the web app today.
:::

## Your first warehouse is created for you

Every business starts with one warehouse. If your business doesn't have one yet, Fintranzact creates it the first time you open the Warehouses page:

- a premises called **Main premises** (code `MAIN`), and
- a warehouse inside it called **Main warehouse** (code `MAIN`, type Main warehouse), with your business address.

This warehouse becomes the default for everything, so a single-location business never has to think about warehouses at all. Invoices simply take stock out of, and put stock into, Main warehouse.

:::tip[Premises]
Every warehouse belongs to a **premises**, which is a parent grouping. The web app doesn't have a screen to add premises yet, so all your warehouses sit under **Main premises**. The premises name appears on each warehouse card and in the **Premises** column of some inventory reports.
:::

## The Warehouses page

The page has three parts:

1. **Warehouse cards.** One card per warehouse, showing its name, `code · type · premises`, **Units in stock**, and address. The warehouse used as the default for sales carries a green **Default** badge. Inactive warehouses are dimmed and show an **Inactive** badge. Owners and admins see a **Manage** link on each card.
2. **Settings card.** Three business-wide inventory settings:
   - **Selling more than you have**: Allow, Warn or Block. See [How Stock Moves](/inventory/how-stock-moves#selling-more-than-you-have).
   - **Stock valuation**: Average cost or FIFO. See [Stock Valuation](/inventory/stock-valuation).
   - **Default warehouses**: see below.
3. **Stock by warehouse table.** One row per stock item (each variant gets its own row), one column per active warehouse, and a **Total**. The total turns amber with a **low** tag when it is at or below the item's low-stock alert. Use the search box ("Search item or SKU…") to find an item.

:::note
**Units in stock** on a card is a plain sum of quantities, even when items use different units (pieces, kg, metres). Use it as a rough indicator, and use [Godown Summary](/inventory/reports#godown-summary) for stock value per warehouse.
:::

Only owners and admins can change the settings card. Other team members see the current values without being able to change them.

## Adding a warehouse

1. Click **+ Add warehouse**.
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | Notes |
   |---|---|
   | **Name** | Required. For example "Pune godown". |
   | **Short code** | Required, and unique within your business. Converted to capitals (for example `PUNE`). |
   | **Type** | Main warehouse, Godown (default), Shop / store, In transit, or Other. |
   | **Address (optional)** | Street and city. |

3. Click **Add warehouse**.

The **Type** is a label that helps you recognise the warehouse. It doesn't change how the warehouse behaves. For example, an "In transit" warehouse works like any other warehouse.

Only owners and admins can add warehouses. If another team member tries, they see "Couldn't add warehouse".

## Default warehouses

Once you have **two or more active warehouses**, the settings card shows a **Default warehouses** section. A default is the warehouse Fintranzact uses when a document doesn't pick one, and it is preselected in the **Dispatch from** / **Receive into** picker on new documents.

| Default | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Sales & delivery challans** | Sale invoices (including POS and online store orders) and delivery challans. Also holds [unplaced stock](#unplaced-stock). |
| **Purchases** | Purchase invoices and goods receipt notes (GRN) |
| **Sales returns** | Sales returns |
| **Purchase returns** | Purchase returns |
| **Opening stock & adjustments** | Opening stock entered on a new item, the **Adjust Stock** button on an item, and stock numbers edited on the item form |

After changing a dropdown, click **Save defaults**. Only active warehouses can be chosen.

<ForBusiness>
A typical two-location setup: keep **Sales & delivery challans** on your shop, and point **Purchases** at your godown. Supplier deliveries land in the godown; when the shop runs low, move stock across with a [stock transfer](/inventory/stock-transfers).
</ForBusiness>

## Managing a warehouse

Click **Manage** on a warehouse card. A panel opens with three tabs: **Details**, **Locations** and **Access**.

### Details

Edit the **Name**, **Short code**, **Type** and **Address**, then click **Save changes**. The premises can't be changed.

**Making a warehouse inactive.** Click **Make inactive** in the status box. An inactive warehouse:

- can't be picked on documents, transfers, adjustments or stock counts,
- no longer appears as a column in the Stock by warehouse table, and
- stays on the page as a dimmed card, with its history intact. Click **Make active** to bring it back.

You can't make a warehouse inactive while:

- it is a default warehouse. Choose another default first.
- it still holds stock (positive or negative). [Transfer](/inventory/stock-transfers) the stock out first.

**Deleting a warehouse.** Click **Delete** and confirm. You can only delete a warehouse that:

- is not a default warehouse, and
- has **never had any stock movement**. A warehouse with any history can't be deleted; make it inactive instead.

### Locations

Use locations to note where things are kept inside a warehouse: areas, racks, shelves and bins.

1. Under **Add a location**, enter a **Name** (for example "Rack A") and a **Code** (for example `A`). The code must be unique within the warehouse.
2. Choose a **Type**: Area, Rack, Shelf or Bin.
3. Choose **Inside**: **Top level**, or an existing location, to nest it (for example "Area 1 › Rack A › Shelf 2").
4. Click **Add location**.

To stop using a location, click **Retire**. Retired locations are shown struck through and can be brought back with **Restore**.

:::caution[Locations are labels today]
Locations are a written record of your layout. Transfers, adjustments, documents and stock counts all work at warehouse level. You can't yet keep stock in a specific rack or bin, and no report splits stock by location.
:::

### Access

Owners and admins can move stock in every warehouse. The **Access** tab controls what other team members can do in *this* warehouse. Every member of the business is listed. Owners and admins show **Full access**; everyone else has two checkboxes:

| Checkbox | Lets the member |
|---|---|
| **Transfer** | Make [stock transfers](/inventory/stock-transfers) into or out of this warehouse. They need this on **both** warehouses of a transfer. |
| **Adjust & count** | Make [stock adjustments](/inventory/stock-adjustments), post [physical stock counts](/inventory/physical-stock), and post [manufacturing journals](/inventory/manufacturing) at this warehouse. |

Untick both boxes to remove the member's access to that warehouse.

A few things to know:

- These checkboxes only matter for team members whose role already lets them change stock items (for example, a seller manager). Sellers and accountants can't transfer or adjust stock at all, whatever is ticked here.
- Access doesn't hide anything. Members still see every warehouse, its stock and the transfer list.
- Access doesn't apply to invoices, delivery challans, GRNs or returns. Anyone who can create those documents can pick any active warehouse on them.

## Unplaced stock

If your business used Fintranzact before warehouses existed, some of your stock was recorded without saying *where* it is. That stock is called **unplaced**: it's the difference between an item's total stock and the stock recorded in its warehouses.

You don't need to do anything about it:

- Unplaced stock is always shown as part of your **Sales & delivery challans** default warehouse: on its card, in its column of the stock table, in the "in this warehouse" hints on forms, and in the stock shortage check on sale documents.
- The first time a transfer, adjustment, stock count or blocked-sale check touches that item, Fintranzact quietly records the unplaced quantity into the sales default warehouse. Your total stock doesn't change.

:::tip
Because unplaced stock follows the sales default, changing the **Sales & delivery challans** default also changes where not-yet-placed stock appears to sit. If you're setting up a second warehouse, decide your sales default first.
:::

<ForCA>
Unplaced stock only affects *where* quantities show up, never the business-wide totals, so stock valuation, the P&L and the Balance Sheet are unaffected by it.
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to set up warehouses if I have only one shop?**
No. Your business already has **Main warehouse**, and everything uses it automatically. The warehouse picker doesn't even appear on documents until you have a second active warehouse.

**Why can't I delete an old warehouse?**
A warehouse that has ever had a stock movement can't be deleted, because its history is part of your stock records. Move any remaining stock out with a transfer, pick another default if needed, and click **Make inactive**.

**Why is the Default warehouses section missing?**
It only appears once you have at least two active warehouses. With one warehouse, it's the default for everything.

**Can I stop a staff member from selling out of a particular godown?**
Not yet. The **Access** tab controls transfers, adjustments, stock counts and manufacturing. It doesn't restrict which warehouse someone picks on an invoice or delivery challan.

**The "Default" badge is on one warehouse, but purchases go to another. Is that right?**
Yes. The badge marks the **Sales & delivery challans** default. Each document type can have its own default, as shown in the Default warehouses section.
