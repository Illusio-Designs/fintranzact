## Statutory dues

When a payroll run is **posted** to your books, the statutory amounts (employee and employer shares) are booked to their own payable accounts instead of the general ones: **PF and EPS Payable**, **ESI Payable**, **Professional Tax Payable**, **Labour Welfare Fund Payable** and **TDS on Salary Payable**. The entry always balances and posting twice changes nothing. A business without PF never gets a PF account.

Open **Payroll → Statutory dues**. For each run and authority you see what is **accrued**, **paid**, **outstanding** and the **due date** (from your due-day settings; blank when not configured, as for professional tax and labour welfare fund). Overdue amounts are flagged.

**Record payment** after you have paid through your bank or the government portal. Enter the amount, the date, the bank or cash account it came from, the **challan number and date** and a reference (BSR code, TRRN or UTR). It writes *Dr payable account / Cr bank* and a withdrawal on the account. You cannot record more than is outstanding, and a locked period refuses it. **Fintranzact does not pay anything**: it records what you paid.

## Files you upload or hand to your CA

On **Payroll → Filings and registers**, choose an approved run:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **PF ECR file** | A text file in the EPFO ECR 2.0 layout, only for PF members with a UAN: gross and EPF, EPS and EDLI wages, the employee's EPF contribution (including VPF), the EPS contribution, the employer's EPF share, non-contributory days. A member without EPS has zero pension wages and contribution. Members left out (for example without a UAN) are listed. Only offered when the run was calculated with PF |
| **ESIC contribution file** | A CSV with the columns of ESIC's monthly contribution upload: IP number, name, days, monthly wages, reason code, last working day. Covered employees only |
| **Professional tax sheets** | A working sheet per state: employee, gross and tax, with a total |
| **Labour welfare fund sheets** | The same for labour welfare fund |

**Check every file against the portal's current template before you upload it.** The ECR layout and the ESIC columns are the best documented ones we know, and the portals change them. Fintranzact files nothing.

The files contain **UAN, ESIC and PAN numbers in full**, so only an owner, admin or accountant can download them. They are never written to logs.

## Registers

| Register | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Wages register** | A month: each employee's paid days, a column per earning and deduction, gross, deductions and net pay |
| **Attendance register** | A month: each employee, one column per day (P present, A absent, HD half day, WO weekly off, H holiday, L leave) |
| **Leave register** | A leave year: opening or carried forward, accrued, taken, encashed, lapsed and balance for each employee and leave type |
| **Bonus register** | A financial year: Basic and DA earned, the wages after your bonus ceiling and the bonus at your configured percentage. If you have not set a ceiling and percentage the register says so and shows no bonus |
| **Gratuity register** | As on a date: completed years, last drawn Basic and DA, whether the employee has completed the minimum years, and the computed gratuity (15 days for every 26 working days per year, a part year of more than six months counted as a year, up to the configured limit) |

The bonus and gratuity registers are **computed from your payroll data**. Bonus and gratuity are paid from the [Bonus](/help/payroll/bonus), [Gratuity](/help/payroll/gratuity) and [Full and final](/help/payroll/full-and-final) tabs. Four more working registers (employees, deductions and advances, overtime, settlements) are described in [Labour-law registers](/help/payroll/labour-law-registers).
