Open **Payroll → Statutory settings**. Everything the statutory part of payroll uses is **data you can edit**, not fixed in the software: the registrations of your business and, for each **financial year** (April to March), every rate, ceiling, slab and due date.

**Verify with your CA.** Rates and limits change, often with the Budget, and they are your responsibility. Fintranzact ships only a few starting figures; each one is listed in the verification checklist your CA can sign off. Review the settings before your first statutory run and again every year.

## Registrations

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Registered for provident fund (PF)** and the **PF establishment code** | Turns on PF, voluntary PF and EPS. **When it is off, PF and EPS appear nowhere**: not on salary lines, payslips, files, dues or screens |
| **Registered for ESI** and the **ESI code** | Turns on ESI contributions |
| **Professional tax states** | The states you deduct professional tax in. An employee's **state of work** decides which state's slabs apply |
| **Labour welfare fund state** | The state whose labour welfare fund you deduct |
| **Deduct income tax (TDS) on salary** | Turns on TDS under section 192. Needs the business's TAN and the income-tax slabs below |

Only an owner or admin can change the registrations and the rates; an accountant can read them. A change applies to the next run you **calculate**. A run you already calculated keeps what it used (and an approved run never changes).

## Rates and limits for a financial year

Choose the financial year at the top. A run uses the **latest year saved at or before its own**, so last year's figures carry forward until you save new ones. Add a short **last verified** note (who checked and against what) and the date, then **Save**.

Only the sections for the schemes you are registered for are shown.

- **Provident fund**: employee and employer percentages (12 and 12), the EPS share (8.33), the PF and EPS wage ceilings (₹15,000), the rounding, the date from which a new member earning above the ceiling has no EPS, and the age EPS stops (58)
- **ESI**: employee and employer percentages (0.75 and 3.25), the wage ceiling (₹21,000) and the rounding
- **Professional tax, per state**: slabs by monthly gross (above, up to, the tax a month, a different **February** amount where the state needs one, and whether the slab is for men, women or everyone) and the yearly maximum (₹2,500)
- **Labour welfare fund, per state**: how often, the months it is deducted in, and the employee and employer amounts
- **Income tax on salary**: for the new and the old regime the slabs, the standard deduction, the section 87A rebate limit and amount, marginal relief, the cess, the rounding, and the old-regime limits for 80C, 80D and home-loan interest
- **Due dates**: the day of the following month that PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax and labour welfare fund are due, shown on the [Statutory dues](/help/payroll/statutory-files-and-registers) tab
- **Bonus and gratuity registers**: the parameters the computed registers use

### What is left empty on purpose

Fintranzact does not guess figures it cannot be sure of. **State professional tax slabs** (except the ones it starts with, which you must still check), **labour welfare fund amounts** and the **income-tax slabs** start **empty**:

- the screen says **"Slabs not configured: add your state's slabs"** (or the equivalent for labour welfare fund and income tax),
- the payroll run deducts **nothing** for that item and shows a **warning**, so you cannot miss it.

Enter the figures your CA gives you, save, and calculate the run again.
