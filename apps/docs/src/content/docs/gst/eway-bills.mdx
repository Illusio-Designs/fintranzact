An **e-way bill (EWB)** is needed to move goods worth more than ₹50,000. Fintranzact generates EWBs from your existing invoices through the NIC E-Way Bill API, keeps a record of each one, and flags the ones that are close to expiry.

:::note[Web and desktop only]
E-way bills are available in the web app and the desktop app. The mobile app doesn't have them.
:::

## Where to find it

In the sidebar, under **Reports**, click **E-Way Bills**. The menu item appears only for GST-registered businesses.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Superadmin, Admin | Generate, update vehicle, cancel, view |
| Accountant | View only. The action buttons are visible, but the server refuses the action. |
| Seller, Seller Manager | No access. The menu item is hidden. |

## Setting up the connection

1. Go to **Settings → Business** and click **Edit**.
2. In the **Statutory & compliance details** step, under **Compliance features**, turn on **Enable E-Way Bill**.
3. Enter the **Portal ID** and **Portal Password** of the API user you created on the NIC E-Way Bill portal for your GSTIN.
4. Save the business.

The credentials are stored encrypted. The GSP client key comes from the Fintranzact server. If no credentials can be found at all, every action fails with *"E-Way Bill API credentials are not configured. Add your E-Way Bill portal ID and password in business settings, or contact your administrator."*

The same step also has an **E-Way Bill Threshold (₹)** field (*"Leave blank to use the statutory ₹50,000"*).

:::caution[Current behaviour to be aware of]
- **The threshold setting isn't applied yet.** Generation always requires the invoice total to be at least **₹50,000**, whatever you enter in **E-Way Bill Threshold (₹)**.
- **There's no sandbox/production switch for e-way bills in the app.** A connection saved from business settings is created in NIC's **sandbox** (test) mode. Before you rely on the EWB numbers for real consignments, check with whoever runs your Fintranzact server that the connection points to production.
:::

## Generating an e-way bill

1. On the **E-Way Bills** page, click **+ Generate EWB**.
2. Fill in the **Generate E-Way Bill** panel:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Invoice ID** | Yes | The invoice's internal ID (a UUID), **not** the invoice number. The web app accepts invoice links of the form `/invoices?id=…`; the value after `id=` is the UUID. |
| **Vehicle Number** | Yes | Converted to capitals, for example `MH12AB1234` |
| **Distance (km)** | Yes | 1 to 4000 km. Used to work out validity. |
| **Transport Mode** | Yes | Road (default), Rail, Air or Ship |
| **Vehicle Type** | Yes | Regular (default) or Over Dimensional Cargo |
| **Transporter ID (optional)** | No | The transporter's GSTIN or transporter ID |
| **Transporter Name (optional)** | No | |
| **From Pincode (optional)** | No | 6 digits. If blank, your business pincode is used for a sale, or the supplier's pincode for a purchase. |
| **To Pincode (optional)** | No | 6 digits. If blank, the party's pincode is used for a sale, or your business pincode for a purchase. |

3. Click **Generate EWB**.

Before calling NIC, Fintranzact checks that:

- the invoice exists and isn't **cancelled**
- it has at least one line item, and at least one line is **goods**. Service-only invoices are refused with *"E-Way Bill can only be generated for invoices containing goods (not service-only)"*. A line with no linked item counts as goods.
- the invoice total (including GST) is at least **₹50,000**
- the invoice doesn't already have a **Generated** or **Active** EWB

Both **sale** invoices (outward supply) and **purchase** invoices (inward supply) are accepted. A party without a GSTIN is sent as `URP` (unregistered person). Each line is sent with the item's HSN code, so fill in HSN codes on your items. A line whose item has no HSN is sent with an empty HSN.

### How validity is calculated

**Valid Upto** is calculated from the time of generation:

| Vehicle type | Validity |
|---|---|
| Regular | 1 day for every 100 km (or part of it) |
| Over Dimensional Cargo | 1 day for every 20 km (or part of it) |

For example, 250 km on a regular vehicle gives 3 days.

## The E-Way Bills page

### Summary cards

Four cards sit across the top. Clicking one filters the list.

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Generated** | EWBs with status Generated |
| **Active** | EWBs with status Active |
| **Expiring Soon** | Generated or Active EWBs whose validity ends within the next 24 hours. Clicking it opens the **Expiring Soon** tab. |
| **Expired** | EWBs with status Expired |

### All E-Way Bills tab

You can filter by status: **All Statuses**, **Generated**, **Active**, **Cancelled** or **Expired**. The table has these columns: **EWB Number**, **Invoice**, **Party**, **Mode**, **Vehicle**, **Generated**, **Valid Upto**, **Status**. It shows 20 bills per page, newest first.

**Valid Upto** is colour-coded:

- green: more than 24 hours left
- amber, with *(Nh left)*: less than 24 hours left
- red: the validity has passed

Hover over a row to see its actions:

| Action | Shown when |
|---|---|
| **View** | Always, when the EWB is linked to an invoice. Opens **E-Way Bill Details**. |
| **Update** | Status is Generated or Active |
| **Cancel** | The EWB isn't cancelled or expired and was generated less than 24 hours ago |

**E-Way Bill Details** shows the EWB number, status, generated date, valid-upto date, transport mode, vehicle number, distance, from and to state, the cancellation reason (if any), and a **Vehicle Update History** list.

### Expiring Soon tab

This tab lists Generated and Active EWBs that expire within 24 hours, soonest first. The **Time Left** badge turns red when less than 8 hours remain. Each row has an **Update Vehicle** button.

## E-way bill statuses

| Status | When it's set |
|---|---|
| **Generated** | Straight after a successful generation |
| **Active** | After the validity has been extended (see below) |
| **Cancelled** | After a successful cancellation |
| **Expired** | Reserved for bills past their validity |

:::note
Fintranzact doesn't currently switch a bill to **Expired** automatically when **Valid Upto** passes. Use the red **Valid Upto** date to spot bills that have run out.
:::

## Updating the vehicle (Part-B)

Use this when goods are moved to another vehicle because of a breakdown, transshipment or a change of route.

1. Click **Update** on a row, or **Update Vehicle** on the Expiring Soon tab.
2. In **Update Vehicle (Part-B)**, enter the **New Vehicle Number**.
3. Optionally enter **From Place (optional)**, the city where the vehicle changed.
4. Choose a **Reason**: *Breakdown*, *Transshipment*, *First Time* or *Others*.
5. Click **Update Vehicle**.

The update is sent to NIC with your business's state as the "from" state. The new vehicle number is saved on the bill and added to its **Vehicle Update History**. If NIC returns a new validity date, **Valid Upto** is updated too. You can't update the vehicle on a Cancelled or Expired bill.

## Cancelling an e-way bill

1. Click **Cancel** on the row. It only appears within 24 hours of generation.
2. Choose a **Cancellation Reason**: *Data Entry Mistake*, *Order Cancelled* or *Others*.
3. Click **Cancel EWB**.

After 24 hours the server refuses with *"E-Way Bill cannot be cancelled after 24 hours of generation"*. Once a bill is cancelled you can generate a new EWB for the same invoice.

## Extending validity

The API has a validity-extension call (`ewayBill.extend`). It's allowed only from 8 hours before to 8 hours after expiry, and it needs the vehicle number, current place, pincode and remaining distance. A successful extension sets the status to **Active**. The web page doesn't have a button for it yet.

<ForBusiness>
**A simple routine for dispatch:** raise the invoice first, then generate the EWB before the truck leaves. Keep the **Expiring Soon** tab open on busy days. If a consignment won't reach in time or the truck breaks down, use **Update Vehicle** straight away instead of waiting until the bill has expired.
</ForBusiness>

<ForCA>
:::tip[Points to check for clients]
- The ₹50,000 check uses the **invoice total including GST**, and it can't be lowered or raised from settings yet.
- Service-only invoices are blocked. A mixed goods and services invoice is allowed, and all of its lines are sent.
- Confirm the client's EWB connection isn't still in sandbox mode. The app has no toggle for it.
- Cancelling an EWB doesn't cancel the invoice. If the supply itself was called off, cancel the invoice too so that it drops out of GSTR-1.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I get the Invoice ID the form asks for?**
It's the invoice's internal UUID, not the number printed on the invoice. Invoice links in the web app look like `/invoices?id=…`; copy the part after `id=`.

**Why am I told the invoice is below the ₹50,000 threshold when my setting says ₹25,000?**
The **E-Way Bill Threshold (₹)** field in business settings isn't used during generation yet. Fintranzact always applies ₹50,000 to the invoice total.

**Can I generate an EWB for a purchase invoice?**
Yes. The same rules apply, and the bill is sent as an inward supply with the supplier's details as the "from" side.

**The bill has passed its Valid Upto date but still shows Generated. Is that right?**
Yes. Statuses don't change to Expired automatically. The red **Valid Upto** date tells you the bill has run out.

**How do I extend an e-way bill?**
The web page doesn't have an extend button yet. You can extend through the NIC e-way bill portal, or through the `ewayBill.extend` API within 8 hours either side of expiry.

**Can an accountant generate or cancel EWBs?**
No. Accountants can view e-way bills, but generating, updating and cancelling need an Admin or Superadmin.
